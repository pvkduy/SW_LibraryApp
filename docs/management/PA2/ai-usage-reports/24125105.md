## 5. Assessment

### Where AI Added Real Value

- **Information Mapping:** The AI was highly effective at synthesizing scattered information from the Project Proposal into the rigid, tabular RUP formats required for the Vision Document.
- **Accelerated Learning:** It saved significant time by quickly explaining complex tooling (Spec Kit) and architectures (RAG) within the specific context of my library project, rather than providing generic web answers.

### Where AI Was Unreliable / Limitations

- **Context Dependency:** The AI cannot generate accurate project-specific content without being heavily prompted. I had to explicitly feed it the PA guidelines, templates, and the exact proposal text to get usable results.
- **Decision Making:** The AI cannot make business or design decisions. When brainstorming, it offered multiple paths, but I had to manually evaluate and select the features that fit our 3-sprint coding capacity.
