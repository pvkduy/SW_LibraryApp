### **What went well**

- The use case list was defined first, so the diagrams, specifications and prototypes could proceed from it.  
- All use case specifications were completed and cross-reviewed between Duy and Thuận.  
- Every task had one performer and one reviewer, and all work was tracked on Jira.

- All five members attended the planning, Scrum, and review meetings.

### **What went wrong**

- The admin-side scope was too large, and some flows needed team revision to be easier to use.  
- Feature reductions during UI design forced extensive rework of the Use Case Diagram late in the sprint.

### **Lessons learned**

- Validate scope against UI prototypes early, before finalizing diagrams and specifications.  
- Reduce complex flows during planning rather than after specifications are written.  
- Agree on template conventions, such as what belongs in actor steps versus backend logic, before splitting the writing work.  
- Keep the diagrams, specifications, prototypes and Vision Document in sync through traceability checks.

### **Carry-over to next Sprint**

Reduce admin-side scope and synchronize with Vision Document
