## 1. Scope

AI was used for the PA3 document work: the `Changes.md` rewrite, the use-case
diagram set, and the Use-Case Diagram document itself. I supplied the source
material (Vision Document, Use-Case Catalogue, the UML@Classroom slides) and
made every content and scope decision; the AI answered questions, checked the
diagrams against the catalogue, and rendered the files so I could inspect them.

The most valuable use was **checking, not writing**: confirming that 32 use
cases were covered exactly once, and catching that the first diagram set was not
UML notation at all. The least reliable outputs were **tooling claims** (Mermaid
version support), which were wrong until I settled it by rendering the files.

**About this log.** The prompts below are **representative samples, not an
exhaustive log** — one for each kind of question that actually changed the
document. Prompts that only asked the AI to perform a mechanical edit are
omitted, because the decision behind them is already visible in the artefact.
The *Purpose* column is the kind of check (find information, validation,
coverage, decision), so the table can be scanned by need rather than read in
order.
