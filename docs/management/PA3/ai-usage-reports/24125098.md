## 2. Student Role, AI Proposal Filtering & Student Decisions

I maintained strict ownership over the project requirements, architecture boundaries, and final document contents. While the AI served as a rapid generator and analytical checker, I actively reviewed, filtered, and discarded several AI proposals that did not align with our team's realistic workload, system design, or course guidelines.

### Ideas Proposed by AI that I Filtered Out or Rejected:

- **Rejection of Functional Decomposition in Use Cases:**  
  When asked to plan the reader use cases, the AI initially proposed splitting `UC-06` (Discover and inspect books) and `UC-10` (Check out books) into micro use-cases such as *"Filter by Category"*, *"Sort by Popularity"*, *"Select Loan Duration"*, and *"Validate Cart"*.  
  *My Action:* I rejected this decomposition because slide 25 of `01_UseCaseDiagram_slides_2015.pdf` explicitly warns: *"The various steps are part of the use cases, not separate use cases themselves -> NO functional decomposition"*. I forced the AI to keep these steps inside the Basic and Alternative Flows of `UC-06` and `UC-10`.
- **Rejection of Over-Engineered Online Payment Gateways:**  
  In `UC-05` (Membership) and `UC-13` (Pay a charge), the AI proposed incorporating 3D-Secure multi-step card verification and external webhook idempotency handlers.  
  *My Action:* I removed these flows because our project specification (`SHELFHELP_FULLSPEC.md`) explicitly specifies a simulated payment gateway (`MOCK_PAYMENT_SUCCESS` / `MOCK_PAYMENT_FAILED`) for academic demonstration. I replaced the complex webhooks with direct synchronous simulation responses.
- **Rejection of Complex LLM Conversational Chaining in UC-17:**  
  In `UC-17` (Get research recommendations), the AI suggested an interactive multi-turn clarifying dialogue where the AI asks follow-up questions before searching.  
  *My Action:* I discarded this in favor of a single-query RAG search pipeline with catalog availability filters, which matches our Sprint 3 backend spike capacity.
- **Filtering Redundant Separate Changes Files:**  
  For Task A, the AI initially suggested creating separate individual change files scattered across subfolders.  
  *My Action:* I pointed out that the assignment PDF specifically mandates: *"Use a single Changes.md file per PA submission, with separate sections for each revised document"*. I directed the creation of a centralized, consolidated `docs/management/PA3/Changes.md`.
