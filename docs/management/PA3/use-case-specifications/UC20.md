## 1. Use-Case Name

**UC-20 - Hand over requested books** (Traces to: `FEAT-09`, `FEAT-21`, `FEAT-22`, `FR-CIRC-01`, `FR-CIRC-02`, `FR-CIRC-07`, `FR-COND-01`, `FR-LOAN-07`)

### 1.1 Brief Description

This use case enables a **Librarian (Admin)** at the service counter to verify a Student's borrowing request, validate physical copy barcodes, inspect and document the outgoing physical condition of the book (with optional photos), collect any required counter fee for Premium items, and formally execute the handover. Upon confirmation, the loan state transitions from ready-for-pickup to active (`borrowed`), the physical copy status transitions to `borrowed`, and the calculated due date is stamped to 23:59:59 local time based on the student's membership borrowing privileges.

### 1.2 Actors

- **Primary Actor:** Librarian (Admin)
- **Secondary Human Actor:** Student (Reader)
- **Supporting Actor:** Supabase Storage (Condition photos bucket), Notification Service

## 2. Flow of Events

### 2.1 Basic Flow

This use case begins when a **Student** arrives at the library service desk to pick up reserved or checked-out books.

1. The **Librarian** opens the circulation desk interface at `/admin/circulation` and selects the "Ready for pickup" tab.
2. The **Student** presents their Student ID card or states their alphanumeric Pickup Code.
3. The **Librarian** searches by Student ID, Student Name, Copy Barcode, or Pickup Code.
4. The **System** locates and displays the active borrowing request containing the student's profile summary (full name, student ID, account standing `active`, total outstanding debt `0 VND`, and active membership plan tier) and the list of staged items ready for pickup with assigned copy barcodes and titles.
5. The **Librarian** retrieves the physical book from the staging shelf and scans its unique Code 128 copy barcode label using a handheld barcode scanner.
6. The **System** verifies that the scanned barcode matches the exact copy allocated to the request item.
7. The **Librarian** inspects the physical book, verifies its condition, and selects the outgoing condition rating (`good`, `fair`, `poor`, or `damaged`) on the item row. The Librarian may enter an optional condition note or attach up to 3 photos of existing wear.
8. The **Librarian** clicks the "Confirm Handover" button.
9. The **System** executes a server-side transaction that:
   - Validates that the student's account status is `active` with zero outstanding debt (`debt == 0`).
   - Validates that the pickup deadline has not expired.
   - Updates the loan item status to `borrowed` and stamps `borrowed_at = now()`.
   - Computes the due date according to membership policy (e.g., current date + 14 or 28 calendar days) and stamps `due_date` at 23:59:59 Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh local time.
   - Updates the physical copy status from `held` to `borrowed`.
   - Records an immutable condition snapshot on the loan item.
   - Updates book availability counters (`total_copies`, `borrow_count = borrow_count + 1`).
10. The **System** displays a visual confirmation banner: _"Handover confirmed successfully. Loan due on [Due Date]."_
11. The **System** dispatches an in-app and email notification to the student confirming the handover and providing the return due date. The Librarian hands the physical book to the Student. The use case ends.

### 2.2 Alternative Flows

#### 2.2.1 Student Account Blocked by Outstanding Debt or Inactive Status

_Branching point: At Step 4 of the Basic Flow, if the Student's account status is `restricted` or has unpaid charges (`debt > 0`)._

1. The **System** disables the "Confirm Handover" button, displays a prominent red warning banner, and specifies the exact blocking cause:
   - _"Outstanding debt: [Amount] VND. Settle outstanding charges first."_ or
   - _"Account status is restricted due to overdue loan / loss case."_
2. The **Librarian** informs the Student of the blocking reason.
3. If the student has unpaid charges and wishes to pay at the counter, the Librarian invokes [Extension Point 6.1](#61-settlement-of-counter-payment) to record payment.
4. Once the debt is cleared and the account is restored to `active`, the **System** re-enables the handover controls.
5. The flow resumes at Step 5 of the Basic Flow.
6. If the student cannot clear the hold immediately, the Librarian suspends the handover, leaving the books in held state until the pickup deadline. The use case ends.

#### 2.2.2 Expired Pickup Deadline

_Branching point: At Step 4 of the Basic Flow, if the 48-hour pickup window has elapsed._

1. The **System** displays an error status indicating that the pickup deadline expired.
2. The **System** blocks handover confirmation, displaying: _"Pickup deadline passed. Request has expired."_
3. If the background expiry job has already processed the hold, the copy status has returned to `available` (or promoted to the next reservist).
4. The **Librarian** informs the Student that the hold has expired and returns the physical book to the general collection or processing shelf. The use case ends.

#### 2.2.3 Physical Barcode Mismatch or Wrong Copy Selected

_Branching point: At Step 6 of the Basic Flow, if the scanned copy barcode does not match the allocated copy ID on the pickup record._

1. The **System** plays an audible warning chime and displays a barcode mismatch alert: _"Scanned copy [Barcode] does not match staged copy [Allocated Barcode] for this title."_
2. The **System** keeps the handover confirmation disabled.
3. The **Librarian** checks the shelf staging bay, locates the correct physical book copy bearing the matching barcode, and rescans.
4. The flow resumes at Step 6 of the Basic Flow.

#### 2.2.4 Counter Fee Settlement Required for Non-Member Premium Request

_Branching point: At Step 4 of the Basic Flow, if the pickup item is a Premium collection book requested by a non-member who chose "Pay at Counter"._

1. The **System** flags the item with status `awaiting_payment` and shows the required Premium checkout fee (e.g., `15,000 VND`).
2. The **Librarian** informs the student of the required fee and receives cash or direct counter payment.
3. The **Librarian** enters the collected amount into the counter payment drawer and clicks "Record Counter Payment and Handover" (`FR-CIRC-07`).
4. The **System** validates that the collected amount exactly equals the fee owed, generates a formal Receipt (`FR-RCPT-01`), marks the fee as paid, and proceeds with handover in one consolidated transaction.
5. The flow resumes at Step 8 of the Basic Flow.

#### 2.2.5 Partial Handover of Multi-Item Request

_Branching point: At Step 4 of the Basic Flow, if the Student requested multiple books but only a subset is being collected today (e.g., student forgot tote bag or one book is missing from staging)._

1. The **Librarian** checks only the item checkboxes for the physical books present and ready for handover.
2. The unselected items remain in `ready_for_pickup` status with their original 48-hour pickup expiration timers intact.
3. The **Librarian** clicks "Hand Over Selected Items (N)".
4. The **System** confirms handover only for the checked items, updating their individual loan records and copies to `borrowed`.
5. The **System** retains the remaining items under the student's pickup queue. The use case ends.

## 3. Special Requirements

### 3.1 Security and Role Verification

- Handover operations require authenticated staff privileges with role `admin` or `librarian` (`FR-AUTH-05`). Student users cannot trigger handover endpoints.
- Server-side API endpoints must re-verify student eligibility, account status, and debt balance before committing, preventing client-side bypass.

### 3.2 Transaction Atomicity and Idempotency

- All database mutations for a handover (loan state, copy state, condition record, inventory transaction, counter receipt) must execute within a single PostgreSQL ACID transaction (`DEC-01`).
- The interface and backend must enforce idempotency: double-clicking the "Confirm Handover" button executes exactly one handover mutation (`TS-20`).

### 3.3 Condition Tracking and Media Storage

- Outgoing physical condition must be explicitly recorded (`good`, `fair`, `poor`, `damaged`).
- Photos uploaded at handover must be stored in the private `loan-conditions` Supabase storage bucket with restricted access policies (`FR-COND-01`).

### 3.4 Timezone and Due Date Calculation Rules

- All timestamps and due dates must strictly operate in `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh` timezone.
- Regardless of the exact hour of handover, the loan's calculated due date must expire at exactly 23:59:59 local time on the target calendar day (`DEC-47`).

## 4. Preconditions

### 4.1 Librarian Authentication

The Librarian is logged into the administrative portal with an active session holding valid circulation rights.

### 4.2 Hold Status Precondition

The requested books are physically staged, allocated to the student, and currently in `ready_for_pickup` status with copies marked `held`.

---

## 5. Postconditions

### 5.1 Loan Activated and Copy Borrowed

- Loan item records transition to `borrowed` with valid `borrowed_at` and `due_date` timestamps.
- Physical copies transition to `borrowed` status.
- Recorded condition notes and wear photos are attached to the loan record.
- Any paid counter fees have generated official immutable receipts.
- Student receives loan confirmation notification.

### 5.2 Handover Denied or Deferred

- If blocked by debt, expiration, or student cancellation, no loan is activated. Copies remain `held` or return to inventory per policy.

---

## 6. Extension Points

### 6.1 Settlement of Counter Payment

- **Location:** Step 3 of Alternative Flow 2.2.1, or Step 3 of Alternative Flow 2.2.4.
- **Trigger:** When an outstanding charge, overdue fine, or Premium fee must be settled immediately at the desk before handover.
- **Behavior:** The use case invokes `UC-13 (Pay a charge)` / `UC-23 (Manage receipts and reversals)` to record cash/counter payment, generate an immutable receipt, and clear the student's debt hold.
