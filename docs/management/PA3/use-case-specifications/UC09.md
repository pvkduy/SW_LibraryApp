## 1. Use-Case Name

**UC-09 - Manage borrowing cart** (Traces to: `FEAT-08`, `POL-04`, `POL-05`, `FR-CART-01`, `FR-CART-02`, `DEC-05`, `DEC-22`, `DEC-49`)

### 1.1 Brief Description

This use case enables an authenticated Student (Reader) to assemble and manage a selection of library titles in a borrowing cart prior to checkout. A student can add titles that currently have available copies, review cart lines, choose loan durations permitted by their membership status (e.g., 7 days for non-members; 7 or 14 days for active members), view itemized borrowing fees (such as non-member Premium collection fees), inspect their remaining borrowing allowance, and remove unwanted or out-of-stock items. The borrowing cart provides a temporary staging container and does not reserve physical stock until formal checkout is executed.

### 1.2 Actors

- **Primary Actor:** Student (Reader)
- **Supporting Systems:** ShelfHelp Database (`cart_items`, `books`, `copies`, `memberships`), Supabase Realtime

## 2. Flow of Events

### 2.1 Basic Flow

#### 2.1.1 Adding an Available Book to Cart

This sub-flow begins when an authenticated **Student** is viewing a book on the Book Detail page (`/books/:bookId`) or catalog search list.

1. The **Student** views an available book and clicks the "Add to Cart" button.
2. The **System** performs real-time entitlement and validation checks:
   - Verifies the student account is `active` (not restricted, inactive, or in debt hold).
   - Verifies that the book currently has at least one copy in `available` status.
   - Verifies the book is not already present in the student's cart (`cart_items`).
   - Verifies the student does not already possess an active loan item or open request for this same book (`POL-04`, `CHK026`).
   - Computes the student's active counted items (`awaiting_payment`, `payment_failed`, `ready_for_pickup`, `borrowed`, `overdue`, plus ready reservations) and verifies that adding this book will not exceed the permitted concurrent item limit (3 for non-members, snapshot limit up to 7 for members; `DEC-22`).
3. The **System** inserts a new record into `cart_items` with the student's `user_id`, `book_id`, and default duration (7 days).
4. The **System** increments the cart badge counter in the navigation bar and displays a success toast notification: _"Added to cart."_ The sub-flow ends.

#### 2.1.2 Reviewing and Modifying Cart Items

This sub-flow begins when the **Student** navigates to the Cart page (`/cart`).

1. The **Student** clicks the cart icon in the navigation header.
2. The **System** queries `cart_items` for the current user and joins current catalog metadata, collection tier (`standard` vs. `premium`), and real-time physical copy availability.
3. The **System** evaluates the student's current active entitlements:
   - For Non-Members: Loan duration option is fixed at `7` days. Premium titles incur a borrowing fee of 5,000 VND per 7 days. Standard titles are free (0 VND).
   - For Active Members: Loan duration options include `7` or `14` days. Both Standard and Premium titles are free (0 VND).
4. The **System** displays the cart interface presenting:
   - Itemized lines with cover thumbnail, title, author, collection badge (Standard / Premium), and loan duration selector.
   - Borrowing limit progress meter (e.g., _"2 of 3 allowed items selected"_).
   - Fee breakdown showing per-line fees and total payable amount (in VND).
   - For non-members with Premium items: An upsell banner highlighting: _"Become a member to borrow Premium titles for free and unlock 14-day loans."_
   - Action controls: "Remove" button per item, and a primary "Proceed to Checkout" button.
5. The **Student** may change the loan duration dropdown on eligible lines (e.g., changing from 7 days to 14 days if an active member).
6. The **System** updates the stored duration in `cart_items` and recalculates any duration-dependent fees.
7. If the student wishes to remove an item, they click the "Remove" icon on the corresponding row.
8. The **System** deletes the item from `cart_items`, recalculates total fees and item counts, and refreshes the cart view. The sub-flow ends.

### 2.2 Alternative Flows

#### 2.2.1 Simultaneous Borrowing Limit Exceeded

_Branching point: At Step 2 of Sub-flow 2.1.1, if adding the book would cause the total number of counted items plus cart items to exceed the student's maximum allowed limit._

1. The **System** calculates `current_counted_items + cart_items_count >= max_allowed_limit` (where limit is 3 for non-members or snapshot limit e.g. 7 for members; `DEC-22`).
2. The **System** blocks the addition and disables the "Add to Cart" button.
3. The **System** presents a notification: _"Borrowing Limit Reached: You may only borrow up to [Limit] items simultaneously. Please return outstanding books or remove items from your cart before adding more."_
4. The cart remains unchanged, and the use case ends.

#### 2.2.2 Duplicate Active Loan or Request for Same Title

_Branching point: At Step 2 of Sub-flow 2.1.1, if the student already has an active loan item, pickup request, or awaiting payment request for the identical title._

1. The **System** detects a matching record for `(user_id, book_id)` in `loan_items` with an active status (`awaiting_payment`, `payment_failed`, `ready_for_pickup`, `borrowed`, `overdue`).
2. The **System** prevents duplicate cart addition (`POL-04`, `CHK026`).
3. The **System** displays an explanatory inline error: _"You already have an active loan or pickup request for this title. Multiple concurrent loans of the same book are not permitted."_
4. The flow terminates without updating `cart_items`.

#### 2.2.3 Item Unavailable or Stock Depleted Before Cart Addition

_Branching point: At Step 2 of Sub-flow 2.1.1, if all physical copies of the book are checked out, held, or under repair at the moment the student clicks "Add to Cart"._

1. The **System** verifies that `available_copies == 0`.
2. The **System** rejects the cart insertion and updates the button on the Book Detail view to "Unavailable".
3. The **System** displays a warning message: _"This title is currently out of stock."_
4. The **System** offers alternative actions: "Reserve Book" (for members) or "Notify Me When Available" (Wishlist). The flow ends.

#### 2.2.4 Item in Cart Becomes Unavailable (Stock Loss)

_Branching point: At Step 4 of Sub-flow 2.1.2, if a book currently in the student's cart has its last available copy claimed or held by another patron before checkout._

1. The **System** detects via real-time inventory query that an item in `cart_items` now has 0 available copies.
2. The **System** flags the affected cart row with a prominent warning badge: _"No longer available - Claimed by another borrower"_.
3. The **System** disables the "Proceed to Checkout" button and presents an instructional alert: _"One or more items in your cart are currently out of stock. Please remove unavailable items to continue."_
4. The **Student** clicks "Remove" on the out-of-stock line.
5. The **System** removes the record from `cart_items`, re-enables "Proceed to Checkout" for remaining valid items, and clears the alert banner. The flow resumes at Step 4 of Sub-flow 2.1.2.

#### 2.2.5 Account Gated by Debt Hold or Suspension

_Branching point: At Step 2 of Sub-flow 2.1.1 or Step 4 of Sub-flow 2.1.2, if the student incurs an overdue fine, enters debt hold (`outstanding_balance > 0`), or is restricted._

1. The **System** checks the account gating matrix (`POL-02`, Section 6.1).
2. The **System** blocks cart additions or disables checkout progression.
3. The **System** presents a blocking banner: _"Debt Hold Active: You have outstanding fines or fees totaling [Amount] VND. Please settle your library charges before requesting new books."_
4. The **System** provides a direct link to the payment screen (`/charges`, `UC-13`). The use case ends.

## 3. Special Requirements

### 3.1 Stock Holding and Cart Semantics

- As established in architectural policy `DEC-49` and `FR-CART-01`, **the borrowing cart does not hold or reserve physical copies**.
- Physical stock is strictly locked and held at checkout execution (`UC-10`) using PostgreSQL row-level locks (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`). Consequently, keeping an item in the cart does not guarantee its availability until checkout commits.

### 3.2 Duration Options and Fee Computation

- Loan duration options must dynamically match current entitlements: Non-members are restricted strictly to 7 days; members may select either 7 or 14 days (`DEC-05`).
- Non-member Premium fees are computed as `premium_fee_per_7_days * (duration / 7)`. For default settings (5,000 VND / 7 days), a 7-day loan incurs 5,000 VND. All Standard collection loans are free (0 VND) for both members and non-members.

### 3.3 Real-Time Availability Validation

- Cart line availability should be verified via Supabase Realtime or live re-query upon page mount so that students are immediately notified if another patron holds the final copy.

## 4. Preconditions

### 4.1 Active Authenticated Student Account

The Student is authenticated with an active session (`profiles.status = active`) and is not blocked by account restriction or debt hold.

---

## 5. Postconditions

### 5.1 Cart State Persisted

- The student's chosen items and preferred loan durations are persisted in the `cart_items` table and remain intact across browser sessions until checked out or manually removed.

### 5.2 Cart Item Removed

- The selected book is removed from `cart_items` and the active cart count is decremented.

---

## 6. Extension Points

### 6.1 Proceed to Checkout

- At Step 4 of Sub-flow 2.1.2, when all cart items are valid and available, the student clicks "Proceed to Checkout", invoking `UC-10 (Check out books)`.

### 6.2 Membership Upgrade CTA

- At Step 4 of Sub-flow 2.1.2, a non-member borrower viewing fee calculations may click "Become a member to borrow Premium free", branching to `UC-05 (Manage membership)`.
