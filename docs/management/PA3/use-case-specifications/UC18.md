## 1. Use-Case Name

**UC-18 - Resolve a lost-book case** (Traces to: `FEAT-14`, `FEAT-21`, `FEAT-24`, `POL-08`, `POL-09`, `DEC-17`, `DEC-21`, `FR-AUTH-08`, `FR-CIRC-05`)

### 1.1 Brief Description

This use case governs the resolution of a loan classified as `lost` (automatically after the 60-day overdue threshold, or manually declared). On loss, the copy becomes `lost`, the student is `restricted`, and a `loss_fee` charge equal to the replacement price is created while fines freeze. Resolution follows two paths: return the book, or pay the replacement. A returned copy is brought back through the copy status action in Books → Copies (UC-19); account restoration is performed in the People screen (UC-31).

### 1.2 Actors

- **Primary Actors:** Student (Reader), Librarian (Admin)
- **Supporting Actor:** Payment Provider / Simulator (when paying replacement fees online via `UC-13`)

## 2. Flow of Events

### 2.1 Basic Flow (Path A: Physical Recovery and Return of Lost Book)

1. The **Student** locates the misplaced library book and brings it to the library.
2. The **Librarian** opens the Books → Copies tab and selects the `lost` copy.
3. In the copy detail pane, the **Librarian** changes the copy's status back to `available` (or `damaged`/`under_repair` if the returned condition has degraded) and enters a mandatory reason.
4. The **System** executes the Path A resolution transaction in one step:
   - Updates the loan item status from `lost` to `returned` and stamps `lost_resolved_at = NOW()`.
   - Updates the copy status as chosen.
   - Waives/cancels the unpaid `loss_fee` replacement charge (or, if it was already paid and the return is within the 90-day window, issues a reversal receipt refund).
   - Retains the accrued overdue daily fine charge as an active obligation (`status = unpaid`).
5. The **Librarian** requests the student settle the remaining overdue fine at the counter (or informs the student they can pay online).
6. Once the overdue fine is settled via `UC-13 - Pay a charge`, the student's outstanding debt balance reaches 0 VND.
7. The **Librarian** navigates to the Student Detail page (`/admin/students/:uid`).
8. The **System** evaluates the automated account restoration checklist (`POL-09`, `FR-AUTH-08`):
   - [x] No unresolved lost loans (`lost_resolved_at` is set on all lost items).
   - [x] Total outstanding debt is exactly 0 VND.
   - [x] No active write-off disputes or administrative lock flags.
9. The **System** enables the "Restore Account" button.
10. The **Librarian** clicks "Restore Account", enters a verification note, and confirms.
11. The **System** updates the student's `profiles.status` from `restricted` to `active`, generates an audit log entry (`admin_restored_account`), and dispatches an account restoration notification to the student. The use case ends.

### 2.2 Alternative Flows

#### 2.2.1 Path B: Full Settlement of Replacement Charge

_Branching point: At Step 1 of the Basic Flow, if the student confirms that the physical book cannot be found and wishes to settle the replacement charge._

1. The **Student** logs into the portal and views their "Restricted" banner, or approaches the circulation desk.
2. The **Student** navigates to `/charges` (or the Librarian opens `/admin/students/:uid`).
3. The **System** shows the outstanding `loss_fee` charge equal to the book's catalog replacement price (or system default replacement price) alongside the frozen overdue fine.
4. The **Student** initiates payment for the full replacement charge online via `UC-13 - Pay a charge` (or pays cash at the counter).
5. Upon successful payment verification:
   - The **System** updates `loss_fee` to `paid` and issues a valid financial receipt (`POL-10`).
   - The **System** stamps the loan item with `lost_resolved_at = NOW()`.
   - Per policy `POL-08` and `DEC-17`, the **System** automatically marks the accrued overdue daily fine as `waived`, eliminating double penalty.
   - Outstanding debt balance recalculates to 0 VND.
6. The **System** notifies library staff that the lost book replacement has been paid.
7. The flow resumes at Step 7 of the Basic Flow (Librarian reviews the restoration checklist and restores the account).

#### 2.2.2 Post-Payment Return within 90-Day Refund Window

_Branching point: If a student who previously settled a replacement fee under Path B finds and returns the book within 90 days (`POL-08`, `DEC-17`)._

1. The **Student** brings the previously paid-for lost book back within 90 days of the loss date (`lost_return_refund_window_days = 90`).
2. In the Books → Copies detail pane, the **Librarian** changes the `lost` copy's status back to `available` (or `damaged`) with a reason.
3. The **System** detects that the replacement was already paid within 90 days and displays: _"Eligible for replacement fee refund."_
4. The **System** executes an automated refund reversal (`POL-10`, `FR-RCPT-05`):
   - Generates a negative reversal receipt linked to the original replacement payment receipt.
   - Re-instates the previously waived overdue fine so that late borrowing fees are retained.
   - Marks the Payment `refunded` if paid online (staff returns cash if paid at counter).
5. The student settles the reinstated overdue fine, keeping their account clear. The use case ends.

#### 2.2.3 Post-Payment Return Beyond 90-Day Refund Window

_Branching point: If the book is returned more than 90 days after being declared lost._

1. In the Books → Copies detail pane, the **Librarian** changes the `lost` copy's status back to `available` with a reason.
2. The **System** detects that more than 90 days have elapsed since the loss and alerts: _"Returned beyond the 90-day refund window; policy does not grant an automatic refund."_
3. The copy returns to inventory with no automatic refund reversal. The use case ends.

#### 2.2.4 Manual Lost Declaration by Staff on Borrowed Item

_Branching point: If a student reports a lost book before 60 days overdue, or staff confirms loss during an audit (`DEC-21`, `FR-CIRC-05`)._

1. The **Librarian** navigates to `/admin/circulation` and searches for the borrowed loan record.
2. The **Librarian** clicks "Mark as Lost Manually".
3. The **System** displays a solemn confirmation modal enumerating the consequences:
   - Loan item status changes to `lost`.
   - Copy status changes to `lost`.
   - Student profile is immediately set to `restricted`.
   - A `loss_fee` charge is generated for the replacement amount.
   - Staff is required to enter an explanatory reason.
4. The **Librarian** enters the reason (e.g., _"Student reported textbook water-damaged beyond repair / misplaced on transit"_) and confirms.
5. The **System** executes the state transitions, flags the student profile as `restricted`, records copy inventory changes per UC-22 (Assess damage and write off copies), and issues a notification alert to the student.
6. The flow transitions to Path A or Path B for eventual resolution.

#### 2.2.5 Student Triggers "Request Review" for Account Restoration

_Branching point: When a student has completed their settlement obligations and seeks expeditious account restoration._

1. The **Student** logs into the application and sees the persistent "Account Restricted" banner (`FR-GLOB-01`).
2. The **Student** clicks the "Request Review" button.
3. The **System** checks if `lost_resolved_at` is stamped and outstanding debt equals 0.
4. If conditions are met, the **System** dispatches a high-priority operational alert to the Admin Reminders and Student Oversight queue (`/admin/students`).
5. The **Librarian** opens the alert, reviewing the passing checklist as described in Step 9 of the Basic Flow, and confirms restoration.
6. The use case ends.

## 3. Special Requirements

### 3.1 Rigorous Account Restoration Checklist

- Lifting a `restricted` account status must never occur through an arbitrary single toggle without validation (`POL-09`).
- The "Restore Account" button must remain strictly disabled on the admin interface until every automated check passes:
  1. No loan items in `lost` with an empty `lost_resolved_at`.
  2. Total outstanding user balance equals 0 VND.
  3. No open inventory write-off dispute flags.

### 3.2 Financial Reversals and Waiver Integrity

- Under Path B, waiving the accrued overdue fine must be atomic with the settlement of the replacement fee to ensure students are not doubly penalized for books they have purchased outright.
- Under Path A, cancellation of an unpaid replacement fee must be recorded with reason code `lost_returned`.

### 3.3 Audit Logging of Restriction and Restoration Events

- All transitions into `restricted` and back into `active` must be immutably recorded in the `audit_logs` table, storing the timestamp, student ID, acting librarian ID, and justification notes.

---

## 4. Preconditions

### 4.1 Loan in Lost Status and Account Restricted

The Student has at least one loan item in `lost` status, and their profile is currently marked as `restricted`.

### 4.2 Actor Access and Verification Authority

The Student is authenticated to view charges and request reviews. The Librarian is authenticated with circulation and administrative privileges (`admin` role) to execute returns, waivers, and account restorations.

---

## 5. Postconditions

### 5.1 Case Fully Resolved and Account Restored

- The loan item has a valid timestamp in `lost_resolved_at`.
- All replacement fees or overdue fines are settled (`charges.status = 'paid'` or `waived`).
- The student's profile status is updated from `restricted` to `active`.
- Normal borrowing, renewal, and reservation privileges are fully re-enabled.

### 5.2 Case Pending Verification or Outstanding Debt

- If replacement fees remain unpaid or overdue fines remain outstanding, the student remains in `restricted` status with borrowing privileges blocked.

## 6. Extension Points

### 6.1 Extension to Pay a Charge (UC-13)

- **Condition:** Settle replacement fees or overdue fines.
- **Location:** Step 6 of Basic Flow and Step 4 of Alternative Flow 2.2.1. Invokes `UC-13 - Pay a charge`.

### 6.2 Extension to Manage Student Accounts (UC-31)

- **Condition:** Librarian inspects broader student borrowing history and disciplinary records.
- **Location:** Step 7 of Basic Flow. Interacts with `UC-31 - Manage student accounts`.

### 6.3 Extension to Manage Receipts and Reversals (UC-23)

- **Condition:** Refund of replacement charges when a lost book is returned within 90 days.
- **Location:** Step 6 of Alternative Flow 2.2.2. Invokes `UC-23 - Manage receipts and reversals`.
