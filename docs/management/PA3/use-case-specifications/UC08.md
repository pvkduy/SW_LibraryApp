## 1. Use-Case Name

**UC-08 - Manage a book wishlist** (Traces to: `FEAT-07`, `POL-07`, `FR-SRCH-06`, `DEC-27`)

### 1.1 Brief Description

This use case enables an authenticated Student (Reader) to manage a personal availability-notification wishlist. When a desired book has zero copies available for borrowing, the student can toggle "Notify me when available" to register for automated notifications. When a copy of the wishlisted book transitions back to `available` (through return, batch receipt, or inventory unfreezing) and no priority reservation queue exists for that title, the system dispatches an automated in-app notification and email alert to all students who wishlisted the book. Students can view their complete personal wishlist, check live stock status, navigate directly to book records, and remove items when interest has passed.

### 1.2 Actors

- **Primary Actor:** Student (Reader)
- **Supporting Systems:** Supabase Database & Background Trigger Engine, Notification Service (Email & In-App Alerts)

## 2. Flow of Events

### 2.1 Basic Flow

#### 2.1.1 Adding an Unavailable Book to Wishlist

This sub-flow begins when an authenticated **Student** views a book on the Book Detail page (`/books/:bookId`) that currently has 0 available physical copies.

1. The **Student** observes that the book has 0 available copies and locates the "Notify me when available" (or "Add to Wishlist") button.
2. The **Student** clicks "Notify me when available".
3. The **System** verifies student eligibility:
   - Verifies the user is authenticated and their account status is `active` (not restricted).
   - Counts the student's existing wishlist entries in `wishlist_entries` and confirms that the current count is below the maximum capacity limit (50 titles).
   - Confirms that this book is not already present in the student's personal wishlist.
4. The **System** inserts a new record into `wishlist_entries` containing the student's `user_id`, `book_id`, and `created_at` timestamp.
5. The **System** updates the UI button state to "In Wishlist (Notifications Active)" with a highlighted bell icon and displays a success toast: _"Added to wishlist. You will be notified as soon as a copy becomes available!"_ The sub-flow ends.

#### 2.1.2 Viewing and Removing Wishlist Entries

This sub-flow begins when the **Student** navigates to the Wishlist management interface.

1. The **Student** opens the Reservations & Wishlist page (`/reservations`) and selects the "Wishlist" tab.
2. The **System** retrieves all wishlist records for the authenticated student, joining the current book title, authors, cover image, and live copy availability.
3. The **System** displays the list of wishlisted books (with current item count, e.g., "7 of 50 titles"), each showing:
   - Book cover thumbnail, title, and authors.
   - Real-time availability badge (e.g., "Still Unavailable" or "1 Copy Available Now!").
   - Action buttons: "View Book" (linking to `/books/:bookId`) and "Remove" (trash icon).
4. The **Student** clicks "Remove" on a wishlisted item.
5. The **System** displays a prompt to confirm removal or immediately deletes the row from `wishlist_entries`.
6. The **System** updates the list view, decrements the active count, and displays a confirmation toast: _"Book removed from your wishlist."_ The sub-flow ends.

### 2.2 Alternative Flows

#### 2.2.1 Wishlist Capacity Limit Reached

_Branching point: At Step 3 of Sub-flow 2.1.1, if the student already has 50 titles registered in their wishlist._

1. The **System** detects that `COUNT(wishlist_entries) >= 50`.
2. The **System** blocks the addition and presents an alert modal: _"Wishlist Limit Reached: Your wishlist has reached the maximum capacity of 50 titles. Please remove one or more items from your Wishlist tab before adding new titles."_
3. The **System** provides a direct link button to "Manage Wishlist" (`/reservations?tab=wishlist`). The use case ends without modifying database records.

#### 2.2.2 Book Already in Wishlist (Toggle Off)

_Branching point: At Step 2 of Sub-flow 2.1.1, if the student clicks the button when the book is already present in their wishlist._

1. The **System** identifies that a record already exists in `wishlist_entries` for this `user_id` and `book_id`.
2. The **System** deletes the existing record, toggling the wishlist status off.
3. The **System** updates the button label back to "Notify me when available" and displays a toast message: _"Removed from your wishlist."_ The sub-flow ends.

#### 2.2.3 Book Has Available Copies

_Branching point: At Step 1 of Sub-flow 2.1.1, if the book currently has at least 1 copy in `available` status._

1. The **System** identifies that copies are physically available for immediate borrowing.
2. The **System** hides or disables the "Notify me when available" button on the Book Detail view, displaying the primary "Add to Cart" button instead.
3. If an explicit wishlist request is sent via direct API call, the **System** returns a validation error indicating that titles with available stock cannot be added to the availability notification wishlist. The use case ends.

#### 2.2.4 Account Restricted or Suspended

_Branching point: At Step 3 of Sub-flow 2.1.1, if the student's account status is `restricted` (due to an unresolved lost book or disciplinary hold) or `inactive`._

1. The **System** checks the account gating matrix (`POL-02`, Section 6.1).
2. The **System** blocks the action and displays a notification banner: _"Your account is restricted. Wishlist and notification features are unavailable until all pending account restrictions are resolved."_ The use case ends.

## 3. Special Requirements

### 3.1 Automated Availability Notification Logic

- As defined in library policy `POL-07` and `DEC-27`, when a physical copy transitions from non-available to `available` (e.g., following a book return, write-off cancellation, or new batch receipt):
  - The system checks if there is an active reservation queue for the book. If an active reservation exists, the copy is held for the reservation queue (`UC-14`), and wishlist alerts are **not** triggered.
  - If **no reservation queue exists**, the system immediately triggers an automated notification job (`trigger-wishlist-notifications`) that creates in-app notification records and dispatches email notifications to all users who currently have that title in their `wishlist_entries`.
  - Notification dispatch must be idempotent: multiple copy additions in rapid succession must not generate duplicate alert blasts to the same student within a 24-hour period.

### 3.2 Capacity and Concurrency Constraints

- The per-student wishlist limit is strictly capped at 50 titles (`DEC-27`).
- The addition operation must enforce uniqueness across `(user_id, book_id)` via a database unique constraint to prevent duplicate entries under concurrent tab operations.

### 3.3 Data Retention and Cleanup

- If an administrator marks a student account as `inactive`, the system automatically purges all corresponding entries in `wishlist_entries` as a side effect (`POL-02`, `DEC-43`).

## 4. Preconditions

### 4.1 Active Authenticated Student Account

The Student is authenticated with an active session in ShelfHelp and their account status is `active`.

---

## 5. Postconditions

### 5.1 Wishlist Entry Stored

- A unique row is persisted in `wishlist_entries` linking the student and book.
- The student is registered to receive automated availability notifications upon inventory replenishment.

### 5.2 Wishlist Entry Removed

- The specified row is deleted from `wishlist_entries`.
- Automated availability alerts for this title are discontinued for this student.

## 6. Extension Points

### 6.1 Direct Transition to Cart from Wishlist

- When viewing wishlisted items in Sub-flow 2.1.2 that have become available ("1 Copy Available Now!"), the student may click "Add to Cart", branching to `UC-09 (Manage borrowing cart)`.

### 6.2 Direct Transition to Reservation Queue

- If the student has an active membership and prefers guaranteed queue placement over passive notification, they may invoke `UC-14 (Reserve an unavailable book)`.
