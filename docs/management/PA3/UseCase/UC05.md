## 1. Use-Case Name

**UC-05 - Manage membership** (Traces to: `FEAT-03`, `POL-03`, `FR-MEM-01`, `FR-MEM-02`, `FR-MEM-03`, `FEAT-24`)

### 1.1 Brief Description

This use case allows an authenticated Student (Reader) to view available library membership plans, inspect current membership status and snapshot privileges, purchase a new membership plan, or renew an expiring plan. Membership upgrades reader privileges—granting free borrowing of Premium collection titles, higher simultaneous borrowing limits (e.g., up to 7 items vs. 3 for non-members), longer loan durations (14 days option in addition to 7 days), increased renewals per loan (up to 2 vs. 1), and access to the book reservation queue. Payment is processed online via a simulated payment gateway or recorded over-the-counter by a Librarian (Admin). Each completed purchase records an immutable snapshot of privileges and generates an official financial receipt.

### 1.2 Actors

- **Primary Actor:** Student (Reader)
- **Secondary / Staff Actor:** Librarian (Admin) (assisting via counter purchase)
- **Supporting Actor:** Payment Provider / Simulator (External Payment Gateway API)

## 2. Flow of Events

### 2.1 Basic Flow (Online Purchase / Renewal)

This use case begins when an authenticated **Student** accesses the Membership section of the ShelfHelp web application.

1. The **Student** navigates to the Membership page (`/membership`).
2. The **System** retrieves active membership plan definitions from the `membership_plans` table and queries the student's current membership status, active snapshot privileges, expiration date, and past purchase history from `memberships` and `receipts`.
3. The **System** displays the membership overview screen presenting:
   - Current standing (e.g., Non-Member, Active Member, Upcoming, or Expired) and days remaining.
   - Entitlement comparison table contrasting Non-Member privileges with Member privileges (Standard and Premium fees, item limits, loan durations, renewals, and reservation rights).
   - The fixed yearly Premium membership plan ("Academic Year - 365 Days", price in VND). The Standard (unpaid) rank is shown as the default for comparison, not as a purchasable plan.
   - Past membership purchase receipts with status and viewable receipt details.
4. The **Student** selects a membership plan and clicks "Subscribe" (or "Renew Membership").
5. The **System** evaluates eligibility:
   - Verifies the account status is `active` (not `restricted` or `inactive`).
   - Checks that the student does not have an active membership with more than 30 days remaining (early renewals are restricted to within 30 days before expiration, or after expiration).
   - Determines the effective coverage window: `start_date` is set to the current timestamp for new members or expired plans, or seamlessly stacks the day following the existing plan's expiration for early renewals; `end_date = start_date + plan.duration_days`.
6. The **System** displays an order summary dialog showing the selected plan name, fee, validity dates (`start_date` to `end_date`), and prompts the student to proceed to payment.
7. The **Student** confirms the purchase and chooses "Pay Online".
8. The **System** creates a pending `Payment` record and redirects the student to the simulated payment gateway page (`/pay/:paymentId`).
9. The **Student** reviews the payment details (Merchant, Amount, Purpose) and clicks "Confirm Payment" (or "Simulate Success").
10. The **Payment Provider / Simulator** returns a successful authorization response to the ShelfHelp backend Edge Function (`purchase-membership` / `complete-simulated-payment`).
11. The **System** performs the following transactional operations atomically:
    - Creates a new `memberships` record containing the student's user ID, plan ID, price paid, start timestamp, end timestamp, and a static JSON snapshot of the granted privileges.
    - Updates the `payments` record status to `succeeded`.
    - Generates an immutable, consecutively numbered financial `Receipt` marked with transaction type `membership_fee`, payment method `online`, and status `valid`.
    - Creates an in-app notification and dispatches a confirmation email summarizing the membership activation and benefits.
12. The **System** displays a confirmation screen showing the updated membership status, new expiration date, active snapshot entitlements, and a direct link to view and print the electronic receipt. The use case ends.

### 2.2 Alternative Flows

#### 2.2.1 Early Renewal Outside Allowed Window

_Branching point: At Step 5 of the Basic Flow, if an active member attempts to purchase a renewal plan more than 30 days before their current membership expires._

1. The **System** determines that the remaining duration of the active membership exceeds 30 days.
2. The **System** disables the "Renew" action button and displays an informational banner: _"Your membership is active until [Expiry Date]. Renewal is available starting 30 days before expiration ([Earliest Renewal Date])."_
3. The **Student** acknowledges the notice and remains on the membership overview screen. The use case ends.

#### 2.2.2 Account Restricted or Suspended

_Branching point: At Step 5 of the Basic Flow, if the student's account status is `restricted` (due to an unresolved lost book or disciplinary action) or `inactive`._

1. The **System** checks the account gating matrix (`POL-02`, Section 6.1).
2. The **System** rejects the transaction and displays a blocking alert: _"Account Restricted: Membership purchases and renewals are disabled while your account is under restriction. Please resolve any outstanding lost books or library obligations."_
3. The **System** provides a direct navigation link to the Charges & Fines page (`/charges`) and Account Status overview. The use case ends without creating a payment session.

#### 2.2.3 Simulated Payment Failed or Cancelled

_Branching point: At Step 9 of the Basic Flow, if the student clicks "Simulate Failure" or "Cancel", or if the payment gateway times out._

1. The **Payment Provider / Simulator** registers the unsuccessful attempt or cancellation.
2. The **System** updates the `Payment` record status to `failed` or `cancelled`. No `memberships` or `Receipt` records are created.
3. The **System** redirects the student back to the Membership screen with an alert banner: _"Payment could not be processed. Your membership plan has not been activated. You may retry payment or contact support."_
4. The **Student** may click "Try Again" to restart from Step 6 of the Basic Flow or exit.

#### 2.2.4 Over-the-Counter Membership Purchase

_Branching point: If the Student chooses to purchase or renew a membership directly at the library service counter._

1. The **Student** visits the library circulation desk and requests an in-person membership subscription or renewal, presenting their physical Student ID card.
2. The **Librarian (Admin)** searches for the student record by Student ID or institutional email in the Admin Circulation / Student Details console (`A-08` / `UC-31`).
3. The **Librarian (Admin)** verifies that the student account is active and selects "Register / Renew Membership".
4. The **Librarian (Admin)** selects the desired plan from the active plan list. The **System** displays the plan price, calculated start and end dates (stacking if an active plan has <= 30 days left), and fee in VND.
5. The **Librarian (Admin)** collects the exact cash payment from the student and clicks "Confirm Counter Payment".
6. The **System** records a `Payment` with method `counter`, creates the `memberships` record with the snapshot privileges, and issues an official electronic `Receipt`.
7. The **Librarian (Admin)** prints or emails the receipt to the student. The student's digital account reflects active membership privileges immediately. The use case ends.

## 3. Special Requirements

### 3.1 Membership Snapshot Immutability

- Every purchased membership must store a complete, immutable snapshot of the granted privileges at the exact moment of purchase (`plan_name`, `price_paid`, `max_items`, `loan_day_options`, `renewals_per_loan`, `can_reserve`).
- Subsequent modifications by administrators to global system policies or plan parameters in the `settings` table must **not** retroactively alter the entitlements of an already active membership snapshot (`POL-03`, `POL-18`).

### 3.2 Entitlement Evaluation and Performance

- Entitlements must be resolved server-side through a single canonical function (`resolve_entitlements`) across all workflows (borrowing, reservations, renewals, search action buttons).
- The entitlement resolution query must execute with sub-millisecond overhead by indexing `memberships(user_id, start_date, end_date)` to ensure real-time UI rendering.

### 3.3 Security and Financial Auditability

- Online membership purchases are strictly non-refundable through self-service. Any exceptional administrative refund requires librarian action (`UC-23`), generates a linked reversal receipt (`FR-RCPT-05`), and transitions the membership status to `terminated` (`POL-03`).
- Payment attempts, gateway transaction identifiers, and issued receipt numbers must maintain strict sequential integrity and comply with PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.

## 4. Preconditions

### 4.1 Authenticated Active Session

The Student is authenticated with an active session in the ShelfHelp application. The student account has completed email verification (`profiles.status = active`).

### 4.2 Configured Membership Tiers

The two fixed membership tiers exist in the library configuration: the Standard (unpaid) rank and the yearly Premium membership (Academic Year, 365 days).

---

## 5. Postconditions

### 5.1 Membership Created or Extended

- A new record is inserted into the `memberships` table linked to the student's `user_id`.
- The membership period (`start_date` and `end_date`) is active or queued as upcoming.
- An official financial `Receipt` record is logged in the immutable receipt ledger.
- The student's effective borrowing limit is updated to the snapshot limit (e.g., 7 items), loan duration options expand to include 14 days, renewals per loan increase to 2, and reservation privileges are unlocked.

### 5.2 Transaction Aborted or Unchanged

- If the payment fails or is cancelled, no membership record or receipt is created.
- The student retains their previous membership standing (or non-member tier).
