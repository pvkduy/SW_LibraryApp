## 1. Use-Case Name

**UC-13 - Pay a charge** (Traces to: `FEAT-09`, `FEAT-13`, `FEAT-14`, `FEAT-21`, `FEAT-24`, `POL-05`, `POL-08`, `POL-10`, `FR-LOAN-06`, `FR-CIRC-07`)

### 1.1 Brief Description

This use case allows an authenticated Student (Reader) or Librarian (Admin) to settle outstanding library financial obligations. Charges can originate from overdue daily fines, book damage assessments, lost-book replacement fees, or non-member Premium borrow/renewal fees. The use case supports two primary payment channels: self-service online settlement via the integrated payment gateway simulator, and physical in-person settlement at the library circulation desk recorded by a librarian. Completing payment immediately marks charges as `paid`, generates immutable, sequentially numbered financial receipts, and automatically clears the student's debt hold once all outstanding balances reach zero.

### 1.2 Actors

- **Primary Actors:** Student (Reader), Librarian (Admin)
- **Supporting Actor:** Payment Provider / Simulator

## 2. Flow of Events

### 2.1 Basic Flow (Student Online Payment via Simulator)

This flow describes a Student settling outstanding charges online using the payment gateway simulator.

1. The **Student** navigates to the "Fines & Payments" screen (`/charges`) or clicks an active "Debt Hold" banner on any screen.
2. The **System** displays all outstanding charges grouped by category (`fine`, `damage_fee`, `loss_fee`, `rental_fee`), showing:
   - Associated book title and copy identifier.
   - Calculation breakdown (e.g., days overdue multiplied by daily rate, damage severity tier, replacement price).
   - Date charged and current status (`unpaid`).
   - Total accumulated balance owed.
3. The **Student** selects the charges they wish to pay (or selects "Select All") and clicks the "Pay Online" button.
4. The **System** initiates an online payment session:
   - Calculates the exact total sum for the selected charges.
   - Generates a pending payment record with a unique `paymentId`.
   - Starts a 30-minute payment countdown window.
   - Redirects the browser to the Simulated Payment Gateway page (`/pay/:paymentId`).
5. The **System** renders the payment simulator screen showing merchant name ("ShelfHelp - VNUHCM University of Science"), order reference, charge breakdown, total VND amount, and countdown timer.
6. The **Student** reviews the transaction details and clicks the "Pay" button.
7. The **Payment Provider / Simulator** validates the simulated card/account transaction and issues an approved payment response payload.
8. The **System** invokes the backend Edge Function `complete-simulated-payment` in an atomic database transaction:
   - Verifies the payment session has not expired and belongs to the authenticated user.
   - Updates the payment record status from `pending` to `completed`.
   - Updates the status of all associated `charges` rows from `unpaid` to `paid`, stamping `paid_at = NOW()`.
   - Generates sequentially numbered, immutable `receipts` records (one per paid charge or consolidated transaction) containing receipt number, timestamp, amount, payment method (`online_simulated`), and user reference.
   - Evaluates remaining debt across all user charges; if total remaining outstanding debt is 0, clears the `debt_hold` flag on the student's profile.
9. The **System** displays a payment success screen presenting the receipt summary with links to view and print official receipts (`FR-LOAN-10`).
10. The **System** dispatches an in-app notification confirming payment receipt and debt hold removal (if applicable). The use case ends.

### 2.2 Alternative Flows

#### 2.2.1 Librarian In-Person Counter Payment

_Branching point: At Step 1 of the Basic Flow, if the student opts to pay with cash or card at the physical library circulation counter._

1. The **Student** approaches the circulation counter and presents their physical Student ID card or 6-digit reference code to the **Librarian**.
2. The **Librarian** searches for the student on the Circulation dashboard (`/admin/circulation`) or Student Detail page (`/admin/students/:uid`).
3. The **System** displays the student's outstanding charges, overdue loan items, and current debt hold status.
4. The **Librarian** selects the charges being settled and clicks "Record Counter Payment" (`FR-CIRC-07`).
5. The **System** displays the payment form displaying the exact amount due, payment method (fixed to `counter`), and an optional notes field.
6. The **Librarian** collects the physical payment from the student, enters an optional reference note, and clicks "Confirm Payment".
7. The **System** validates that the entered payment matches the total of the selected charges (full payment required per charge; partial charge amounts are rejected per `POL-10` and `FR-CIRC-07`).
8. The **System** executes an atomic transaction:
   - Creates a `payments` entry marked as `completed` with method `counter` and the recording librarian's user ID.
   - Updates selected `charges` to `paid`.
   - Issues immutable `receipts` rows marked as `manual = false` or `method = counter`.
   - Clears `debt_hold` on the student profile if total remaining balance equals zero.
9. The **System** displays a printable counter receipt modal for the librarian to hand to or print for the student.
10. The **System** updates both Reader and Admin dashboards in real time. The flow completes.

#### 2.2.2 Online Payment Failure / Simulator Rejected

_Branching point: At Step 6 of the Basic Flow, if the simulated transaction fails (e.g., student triggers "Simulate Failure" or simulator times out)._

1. The **Payment Provider / Simulator** returns a payment failure status code.
2. The **System** updates the payment transaction state to `failed` and records the error reason.
3. The **System** displays an error message on `/pay/:paymentId`: _"Payment could not be completed. Your card was not charged."_
4. The **System** presents action options: "Retry Payment", "Switch to Pay at Counter", or "Cancel".
5. If the student selects "Retry Payment", a new payment attempt is initialized, resuming at Step 5 of the Basic Flow.
6. If the student selects "Cancel", the pending payment is cancelled, charges remain `unpaid`, and the student returns to `/charges`.

#### 2.2.3 Payment Session Expiry (30-Minute Timeout)

_Branching point: At Step 5 or Step 6 of the Basic Flow, if 30 minutes elapse without payment confirmation._

1. The **System** detects that the payment window has exceeded 30 minutes (`expired`).
2. If the student attempts to click "Pay" after expiration, the **System** blocks the submission and displays: _"Payment session has expired. Please initiate a new payment."_
3. The pending payment status is transitioned to `expired`.
4. The associated charges remain in `unpaid` status with no financial receipt generated.
5. The student is redirected back to `/charges` where updated balances are recalculated.

#### 2.2.4 Switching from Online Payment to Counter Payment

_Branching point: At Step 4 of the Basic Flow, if the student initially selected online payment but decides to settle in person at the service counter._

1. The **Student** opens their pending payment session or loan item on `/charges` or `/loans`.
2. The **Student** selects the "Switch to Pay at Counter" option (`FR-CART-05`).
3. The **System** checks that the student has not already switched methods previously (switching permitted at most once).
4. The **System** cancels the 30-minute online payment window, transitions the payment method reference to `counter`, and grants a standard 48-hour window for counter settlement.
5. The **System** displays a counter payment voucher and instructs the student to present it to staff at the circulation desk.
6. The flow continues via Alternative Flow 2.2.1.

#### 2.2.5 Partial Selection of Multiple Outstanding Charges

_Branching point: At Step 3 of the Basic Flow, if a student owes multiple discrete charges (e.g., two overdue fines and one damage fee) and chooses to pay only a subset._

1. The **Student** ticks the checkboxes for specific individual charges rather than the entire balance.
2. The **System** validates that each selected charge is settled in full (no partial payments within an individual charge row are permitted per `POL-10`).
3. The **System** processes payment only for the selected charge rows following Steps 4–8 of the Basic Flow.
4. After payment completion, the selected charges become `paid`, but because the student still has remaining unpaid charges, the `debt_hold` flag remains active on their profile.
5. The **System** displays a notification reminding the student: _"Partial settlement complete. You still have an outstanding balance of X VND; debt restrictions will remain in effect until fully settled."_
6. The use case ends.

## 3. Special Requirements

### 3.1 Financial Auditability and Immutable Receipts

- Every successful payment must produce an immutable record in the `receipts` table.
- Receipts cannot be deleted or overwritten. Any correction, refund, or waiver must occur through an explicit reversal receipt referencing the original receipt (`POL-10`, `FR-RCPT-05`).
- Receipts must contain printable attributes: official header, receipt number, student ID, itemized charge descriptions, payment channel, and timestamp.

### 3.2 Idempotency and Anti-Double Payment

- All online payment completions must include a unique idempotency key (derived from `paymentId`).
- Double-clicking the "Pay" button or duplicate callback payloads must not result in duplicate receipts or duplicate balance deductions.

### 3.3 Automatic Debt Hold Clearance

- When an atomic payment transaction commits, the system must immediately query `SUM(amount)` of all remaining `unpaid` charges for the student.
- If the remaining sum is zero, the system must set `profiles.debt_hold = false` within the same transaction, immediately restoring borrowing and renewal privileges.

## 4. Preconditions

### 4.1 Outstanding Unpaid Charges

There exists at least one recorded charge in `unpaid` status associated with the student profile (such as fines, damage penalties, loss replacement fees, or non-member Premium charges).

### 4.2 Actor Authentication

The Student is authenticated into their ShelfHelp account, or the Librarian is authenticated with staff credentials to the Admin portal (`/admin`).

---

## 5. Postconditions

### 5.1 Payment Settled and Receipts Generated

- The selected `charges` records are updated to `paid` status with `paid_at` timestamps.
- Corresponding `payments` and `receipts` rows are persisted in the PostgreSQL database.
- If the student's total outstanding balance reaches 0 VND, `debt_hold` is cleared on `profiles`.
- The student can immediately access services previously blocked by debt hold (such as checkout and renewal).

### 5.2 Payment Aborted or Incomplete

- Charges remain in `unpaid` status.
- No receipt is generated, and the debt hold remains active.
- If a temporary online payment session expired, it is marked as `expired` without financial side-effects.

## 6. Extension Points

### 6.1 Extension from Resolve a Lost-Book Case (UC-18)

- **Condition:** Student resolves a lost book under Path B by settling the replacement fee in full.
- **Location:** Step 4 of `UC-18`. Invokes `UC-13` to process the `loss_fee` charge online or at the counter.

### 6.2 Extension to Manage Receipts and Reversals (UC-23)

- **Condition:** An administrative error occurred, or a lost book was returned after payment within the refund window.
- **Location:** Step 10 of Basic Flow or Step 8 of Alternative Flow 2.2.1. The issued receipt may subsequently be reversed under `UC-23 - Manage receipts and reversals`.
