## 1. Use-Case Name

**UC-24 - Plan book acquisitions** (Traces to: `FEAT-18`, `FEAT-19`, `FR-SUGG-03`, `FR-SUGG-04`, `FR-SUGG-05`, `FR-SUGG-08`, `FR-SUGG-09`, `POL-12`)

### 1.1 Brief Description

This use case enables a **Librarian (Admin)** to review student book acquisition suggestions (viewing their justifications and either rejecting a suggestion or adding its title to a batch) and to assemble acquisition batches. A batch is a hand-made named entry (editable name, plus an expected fulfillment date); each title is added directly from student suggestions with a set quantity (or removed entirely). Batches are stored as drafts until marked `ordered`. Any tabular view is exported via the generic `batch export` button.

### 1.2 Actors

- **Primary Actor:** Librarian (Admin)
- **Supporting Actor:** Notification Service (In-app and Email), University Procurement Department (External stakeholder / document recipient)

---

## 2. Flow of Events

### 2.1 Basic Flow

This use case begins when an authenticated **Librarian (Admin)** reviews the suggestion queue and prepares a new hand-created batch for the upcoming semester.

1. The **Librarian** navigates to the "Suggestions Review" section at `/admin/suggestions` (a master–detail panel over suggestion groups).
2. The **System** displays suggestion groups with the patron justifications of each group's members.
3. The **Librarian** opens a group to inspect the patron justifications.
4. The **Librarian** either (a) enters a **rejection justification** and clicks **Reject**, or (b) selects a **target purchase batch** (a stepper to increase copies) and clicks **Add to batch**.
5. To create a batch, the **Librarian** navigates to `/admin/batches` and clicks "Create Batch".
6. The **System** prompts for the batch details: an editable **name** (e.g., "Semester 2 - 2026") and an **expected fulfillment date**. No supplier/price/budget fields exist.
7. The **Librarian** sets the name and expected date and creates the batch in `draft`.
8. The **Librarian** adds titles directly from the student suggestions, sets the **quantity** per title (a stepper), or removes a title entirely.
9. When the batch is ready, the **Librarian** marks it `ordered` (lines locked). The batch may be kept as a draft until then. The use case ends.

### 2.2 Alternative Flows

#### 2.2.1 Rejection of Student Book Suggestion

_Branching point: At Step 4 of the Basic Flow, if the Librarian rejects a suggested title._

1. The **Librarian** opens the suggestion and enters a **rejection justification** in the justification box.
2. The **Librarian** clicks **Reject**.
3. The **System** marks the suggestion(s) `rejected`, records the justification, and notifies the requesters.

#### 2.2.2 Adding a Title to a Batch with a Quantity

_Branching point: At Step 4 of the Basic Flow, if the Librarian adds a suggested title to a batch._

1. The **Librarian** selects the **target purchase batch**.
2. The **System** shows a **quantity stepper** (increase copies).
3. The **Librarian** sets the quantity and clicks **Add to batch**.
4. The **System** creates (or updates) the batch line with the chosen quantity and marks the suggestion `in_batch`.

#### 2.2.3 Editing and Removing Lines in a Draft Batch

_Branching point: While the batch remains `draft`._

1. The **Librarian** adjusts a line's quantity, or clicks "Remove Title".
2. The **System** applies the change. Removed lines return their suggestions to the suggestion queue.
3. The **System** records the change in the batch activity history.

#### 2.2.4 Cancelling a Batch

_Branching point: If the Librarian cancels a batch with no received lines._

1. The **Librarian** clicks "Cancel Batch" and enters a mandatory reason.
2. The **System** sets the batch `cancelled` and returns linked suggestions to the suggestion queue.

## 3. Special Requirements

### 3.1 Suggestion Grouping and Justifications

- Student suggestions are automatically grouped (normalized ISBN, else title/author similarity); grouped titles contribute each member's justification to the group view.
- The only review actions are **Reject** (with justification) or **Add to batch** (with a chosen quantity).

### 3.2 Batch Line Locking on Order

- Marking a batch `ordered` locks line quantities. Only notes may be edited thereafter.

### 3.3 Notification Delivery and Idempotency

- Rejection and "in batch" decisions notify requesters once per event (`FR-SUGG-09`), idempotently.

### 3.4 Role-Based Access Control

- Only the `admin` role can review suggestions, manage batches, or export tabular views (`FR-AUTH-05`).

---

## 4. Preconditions

### 4.1 Staff Authentication

The Librarian is authenticated with collection-acquisition permissions.

### 4.2 Suggestion Availability

Valid student suggestions exist, from which batch titles are added.

---

## 5. Postconditions

### 5.1 Batch Created and Ordered

- A batch record exists with a name and expected fulfillment date, in `draft` or `ordered`.
- Batch lines carry a set quantity and a `pending`/`received` state.
- Linked suggestions are `in_batch`; requesters are notified.

### 5.2 Batch Aborted or Kept in Draft

- If aborted, no lines are created. If saved as draft, the batch remains editable.
