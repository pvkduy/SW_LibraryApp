## 1. Use-Case Name

**UC-24 - Plan book acquisitions** (Traces to: `FEAT-18`, `FEAT-19`, `FR-SUGG-03`, `FR-SUGG-04`, `FR-SUGG-05`, `FR-SUGG-06`, `FR-SUGG-08`, `FR-SUGG-09`, `POL-12`)

### 1.1 Brief Description

This use case enables a **Librarian (Admin)** to review student book acquisition suggestions, evaluate suggestion demand groups, approve titles into the library's procurement backlog (or reject with mandatory reason codes), assemble approved titles and ad-hoc curriculum recommendations into formal Acquisition Batches, manage order quantities, export versioned purchase orders (CSV/PDF/Excel) for university procurement, and mark batches as ordered (`ordered`), locking item lines for shipment fulfillment. Every decision systematically notifies student requesters while preserving complete audit logs of all batch modifications.

### 1.2 Actors

- **Primary Actor:** Librarian (Admin)
- **Supporting Actor:** Notification Service (In-app and Email), University Procurement Department (External stakeholder / document recipient)

## 2. Flow of Events

### 2.1 Basic Flow

This use case begins when an authenticated **Librarian (Admin)** reviews the suggestion review queue and prepares a new procurement batch for the upcoming academic semester.

1. The **Librarian** navigates to the "Suggestions Review" section at `/admin/suggestions`.
2. The **System** displays all aggregated suggestion groups sorted by student demand count (number of distinct student requesters), showing Group Title, Author, ISBN, Requesters Count, First Submitted Date, and Status (`submitted`, `under_review`).
3. The **Librarian** clicks on a high-demand suggestion group to inspect individual student justifications and notes.
4. The **System** automatically updates the status of all requests in the group from `submitted` to `under_review` (`FR-SUGG-04`).
5. The **Librarian** reviews the academic merit and clicks "Approve Group".
6. The **System** updates the group's status to `approved`, moves it into the "Approved Backlog", and dispatches automated in-app notifications and emails to all students who requested the title.
7. The **Librarian** navigates to the "Acquisition Batches" module at `/admin/batches` and clicks "Create Batch".
8. The **System** prompts for batch header details: `Batch Name` (e.g., _"Semester 2 - 2026 Textbook Procurement"_), `Target Supplier / Vendor`, and `Description / Budget Code`.
9. The **Librarian** inputs the batch details and clicks "Create Draft Batch".
10. The **System** creates the batch in `draft` status and opens the Batch Builder (`/admin/batches/:id`).
11. The **Librarian** clicks "Add from Approved Backlog".
12. The **System** displays the list of approved backlog titles with their demand count.
13. The **Librarian** selects the approved titles, specifies the ordered quantity for each line (e.g., 5 copies), and confirms addition.
14. The **System** creates batch lines, links the underlying suggestion records, and transitions the suggestion statuses from `approved` to `in_batch` (`FR-SUGG-05`).
15. The **Librarian** clicks "Mark Sent to Procurement" (`ordered`).
16. The **System** transitions the batch status from `draft` to `ordered`, locks all batch line quantities against further editing, records an immutable entry in the batch activity log, and notifies all requesters that their requested title has been ordered. The use case ends.

### 2.2 Alternative Flows

#### 2.2.1 Rejection of Student Book Suggestion

_Branching point: At Step 5 of the Basic Flow, if the Librarian decides that a suggested title is inappropriate, out of scope, or too expensive for library acquisition._

1. The **Librarian** clicks "Reject Group" from the suggestion group view.
2. The **System** opens the Suggestion Rejection modal requiring:
   - Mandatory Rejection Reason Code (`FR-SUGG-04`): `already_in_catalog`, `out_of_scope`, `too_expensive`, `duplicate`, or `other`.
   - Optional administrative note / explanation for students.
3. The **Librarian** selects a reason code (e.g., `already_in_catalog`) and, if applicable, searches and selects the existing catalog book record.
4. The **Librarian** clicks "Confirm Rejection".
5. The **System** transitions all suggestions in the group to `rejected`, records the reason code and note, and links the existing catalog book if `already_in_catalog` was selected.
6. The **System** sends rejection notification emails and in-app alerts to all requesters explaining the decision and providing a direct link to the catalog record if already available. The use case ends.

#### 2.2.2 Adding Ad-Hoc Titles Directly to Acquisition Batch

_Branching point: At Step 11 of the Basic Flow, if the Librarian needs to include essential curriculum textbooks requested by faculty that did not originate from student suggestions._

1. The **Librarian** clicks "Add Ad-Hoc Title" on the Draft Batch screen.
2. The **System** provides an ISBN lookup / manual title entry form.
3. The **Librarian** enters the ISBN, Title, Author, Publisher, Estimated Unit Price, and Desired Order Quantity.
4. The **System** validates the input, creates a new independent batch line, and adds it to the batch line items.
5. The flow resumes at Step 11 (to add further titles) or Step 15 of the Basic Flow (to proceed with procurement).

#### 2.2.3 Editing and Removing Lines in Draft Batch

_Branching point: While the batch remains in `draft` status before being sent to procurement._

1. The **Librarian** adjusts requested quantities on existing draft lines (e.g., changing quantity from 5 to 3 copies based on budget constraints).
2. Or, the **Librarian** clicks "Remove Line" for an unwanted title.
3. The **System** removes the line item from the batch.
4. If the removed line was linked to student suggestions, the **System** reverts those suggestions back to `approved` status in the backlog so they are not lost.
5. The **System** logs the modification in the batch activity log.
6. The flow resumes at Step 11 or Step 15 of the Basic Flow.

#### 2.2.4 Batch Order Export with Immutable Versioning

_Branching point: At Step 14 or Step 16 of the Basic Flow, if the Librarian needs to generate a formal purchase order file for the university procurement department._

1. The **Librarian** clicks "Export Batch Order" (`FR-SUGG-06`).
2. The **System** prompts the Librarian to choose the export file format (`CSV`, `PDF`, or `Excel`).
3. The **Librarian** selects the format and confirms.
4. The **System** generates a complete snapshot of all batch lines (ISBN, Title, Author, Publisher, Quantity, Estimated Cost, Line Notes).
5. The **System** archives this snapshot as an immutable export version (e.g., `v1`, `v2`) storing the creating staff member, timestamp, format, and exact line snapshot in the database.
6. The **System** triggers a browser download of the formatted purchase order document.
7. Subsequent exports after line adjustments are automatically numbered sequentially (`v2`, `v3`) allowing staff to re-download historical versions at any time. The use case ends.

#### 2.2.5 Cancelling an Acquisition Batch

_Branching point: The procurement office cancels an entire purchase order or the semester budget is reallocated._

1. While viewing a `draft` or `ordered` batch with no received items, the **Librarian** clicks "Cancel Batch".
2. The **System** displays a cancellation warning dialog requiring a mandatory reason.
3. The **Librarian** inputs the reason and confirms cancellation.
4. The **System** updates the batch status to `cancelled`.
5. Any linked student suggestions in the batch are automatically returned to the `approved` backlog (`POL-12`), and all requesters receive an update notification. The use case ends.

## 3. Special Requirements

### 3.1 Suggestion Demand Aggregation and State Machine

- Student suggestions must be automatically grouped using diacritic-insensitive and accent-insensitive title matching (`FR-SUGG-03`, `POL-12`).
- Suggestion statuses must strictly follow the defined lifecycle: `submitted` $\rightarrow$ `under_review` $\rightarrow$ `approved` $\rightarrow$ `in_batch` $\rightarrow$ `received` (or `rejected` / `withdrawn`).

### 3.2 Batch Line Locking on Procurement Order

- Marking a batch as `ordered` ("Sent to procurement") permanently locks line quantities (`FR-SUGG-05`). Only line notes may be edited thereafter. Line deletions or quantity modifications are blocked by backend schema validations.

### 3.3 Notification Delivery and Idempotency

- Every status decision (approval, rejection, addition to batch, order dispatch) must dispatch email and in-app notifications to all requesters in the group (`FR-SUGG-09`).
- Notifications must be strictly idempotent: if an admin retries an action or updates line notes, requesters must not receive duplicate notifications for the same state transition (`TS-45`).

### 3.4 Role-Based Access Control

- Only users with the `admin` (`librarian`) role can view the suggestions review queue, make approval/rejection decisions, manage batches, or export purchase orders (`FR-AUTH-05`).

## 4. Preconditions

### 4.1 Staff Authentication

The Librarian is authenticated in the administrative portal with permissions to manage collection acquisitions.

### 4.2 Backlog or Title Availability

Valid book suggestions exist in the suggestions queue or the Librarian has bibliographic details for ad-hoc batch creation.

---

## 5. Postconditions

### 5.1 Batch Created and Ordered

- An acquisition batch record exists in `batches` table with status `ordered` (or `draft`).
- Batch line items are locked with designated quantities.
- Linked student suggestions transition to status `in_batch`.
- Immutable export versions and batch activity history rows are committed.
- Requesters receive notification updates.

### 5.2 Batch Planning Aborted or Kept in Draft

- If the operation is aborted before saving, no batch lines are created. If saved as draft, suggestions remain linked in draft state.
