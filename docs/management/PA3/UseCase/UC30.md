## 1. Use-Case Name

**UC-30 - Review due items and send reminders** (Traces to: `FEAT-15`, `FR-ANLY-05`, `FR-NOTIF-04`, `TS-27`, `TS-43`)

### 1.1 Brief Description

This use case enables a Librarian (Admin) to inspect all active loans approaching their due date or currently overdue, organized chronologically into daily or weekly groupings. Staff can expand group headers to review individual borrower details, copy barcodes, loan dates, due dates, and accrued overdue fines. To encourage prompt book returns and minimize student penalties, staff can trigger targeted individual reminder alerts or execute a bulk "Remind All" operation. The system incorporates intelligent anti-spam safeguards that preview recipient numbers and automatically bypass any student who received a reminder within the preceding 24 hours.

### 1.2 Actors

- **Primary Actor:** Librarian (Admin)
- **Supporting Actor:** Notification Service (In-App Notifications & Supabase Realtime)

## 2. Flow of Events

### 2.1 Basic Flow

This use case begins when an authenticated **Librarian (Admin)** opens the Due & Overdue Reminders screen (`/admin/reminders`).

1. The **Librarian (Admin)** navigates to "Due & Overdue" from the circulation administration menu.
2. The **System** queries active loan items in `borrowed` or `overdue` status, retrieving associated book titles, borrower details (`full_name`, `student_id`, `email`), due dates, and last reminder timestamps.
3. The **System** displays the Due and Overdue dashboard:
   - View toggle: Group by "Day" (default) or "Week".
   - Status filters: "All", "Due Soon" (due within the next 2 days per `due_soon_days`), "Overdue (< 7 days)", "Overdue (7-14 days)", "Severe Overdue (> 14 days)".
   - Collapsible group sections ordered chronologically (e.g., "Due Today (Nov 8) - 12 items", "Due Tomorrow (Nov 9) - 8 items", "Overdue: 3 days past due - 5 items").
   - A global "Remind All" action button for the active group or filtered selection.
4. The **Librarian (Admin)** expands a group section to inspect the list of loans.
5. The **System** renders the borrower cards showing student photo/name, student ID, book title, copy barcode, due date, accrued fine amount (if overdue), and an individual "Remind" action button.
6. The **Librarian (Admin)** reviews the borrowing statuses and closes the screen. The use case ends.

### 2.2 Alternative Flows

#### 2.2.1 Toggle Grouping Between Daily and Weekly Views

_Branching point: At Step 3 of the Basic Flow, if the Librarian prefers a broader weekly summary._

1. The **Librarian (Admin)** toggles the grouping control from "Day" to "Week".
2. The **System** re-aggregates the active loan dataset into calendar week buckets (e.g., "Week 45 (Nov 3 - Nov 9) - 34 items", "Week 46 (Nov 10 - Nov 16) - 28 items").
3. The **System** updates group header summaries and loan counts.
4. The flow resumes at Step 4 of the Basic Flow.

#### 2.2.2 Send an Individual Manual Reminder to a Student

_Branching point: At Step 5 of the Basic Flow, if staff desires to send a personal return reminder to a specific borrower._

1. The **Librarian (Admin)** clicks the "Remind" button next to a student's loan entry.
2. If the student has already been reminded within the past 24 hours (`last_reminded_at > NOW() - 24 hours`):
   - The **System** displays a confirmation warning: _"This student was already reminded at {time}. Send another reminder anyway?"_
   - The **Librarian (Admin)** confirms the override or cancels.
3. The **System** dispatches an in-app notification to the student's Notification Center (`UC-28`):
   - For loans due soon: _"Reminder: Your borrowed book '{Title}' is due on {Due Date}. Please renew or return it on time."_
   - For overdue loans: _"Overdue Notice: Your loan for '{Title}' was due on {Due Date}. Fines are accruing at 2,000 VND/day. Please return it to avoid account restriction."_
4. The **System** updates the loan item record setting `last_reminded_at = NOW()`.
5. The **System** displays a toast notification: _"Reminder sent to {Student Name}."_
6. The flow resumes at Step 5 of the Basic Flow.

#### 2.2.3 Execute "Remind All" Bulk Notification with Rate-Limit Filtering

_Branching point: At Step 3 of the Basic Flow, if staff triggers a bulk notification for an entire group or filtered list._

1. The **Librarian (Admin)** clicks the "Remind All" button on the toolbar or group header.
2. The **System** analyzes all distinct students within the target selection and calculates rate-limit exclusions:
   - Total students in selection ($N_{\text{total}}$).
   - Students eligible for notification ($N_{\text{send}}$, having no reminder dispatched in the last 24 hours).
   - Students to be skipped ($N_{\text{skip}}$, having already received a reminder in the last 24 hours).
3. The **System** displays a modal confirmation dialog:
   - _"Send bulk return reminders? Total borrowers: {Total}. Eligible to send: {Eligible}. Skipped (reminded within 24 hours): {Skipped}."_
4. The **Librarian (Admin)** reviews the preview figures and clicks "Confirm Send".
5. The **System** processes the dispatch:
   - Inserts notification records into the `notifications` table for all $N_{\text{send}}$ students.
   - Updates `last_reminded_at = NOW()` across all affected loan items.
   - Logs an audit entry summarizing the bulk reminder action (`FR-SYS-03`).
6. The **System** displays a completion summary banner: _"Bulk reminder complete: {Eligible} reminders dispatched, {Skipped} skipped due to 24-hour cooldown."_
7. The flow resumes at Step 6 of the Basic Flow.

#### 2.2.4 Filter by Overdue Duration and Search Specific Borrowers

_Branching point: At Step 3 of the Basic Flow, if staff investigates severely overdue items or searches a specific student._

1. The **Librarian (Admin)** selects the "Severe Overdue (> 14 days)" filter tab or types a student ID into the search input.
2. The **System** filters the loan list, isolating loans overdue by more than 14 days or matching the specific student ID.
3. The **System** highlights loans approaching the 60-day lost threshold (`lost_threshold_days = 60`) where automated conversion to `lost` status will occur (`FR-INV-05`, `FR-LOAN-08`).
4. The flow resumes at Step 4 of the Basic Flow.

#### 2.2.5 No Due or Overdue Items in Selected Scope

_Branching point: At Step 2 of the Basic Flow, if no loans match the due or overdue criteria._

1. The **System** detects zero matching loan items.
2. The **System** displays a cheerful empty state view: _"No books are currently due or overdue for this selection. All loans are in good standing!"_
3. The "Remind All" button is automatically disabled.
4. The use case ends.

## 3. Special Requirements

### 3.1 Spam Protection and 24-Hour Reminder Cooldown

- The bulk "Remind All" routine must strictly evaluate `last_reminded_at` and automatically exclude any student who was reminded within the past 24 hours (`FR-NOTIF-04`, `TS-43`).
- The modal preview must explicitly declare the count of students who will be skipped before staff can confirm the operation.

### 3.2 Delivery Channels and In-App Priority

- Reminders dispatched via this use case are delivered primarily via the student's in-app Notification Center (`FR-NOTIF-01`).
- Email dispatch is reserved for automated scheduled escalation jobs at 7 and 14 days overdue (`overdue_email_days = [7, 14]`).

### 3.3 Performance and Grouping Aggregations

- Grouping calculations and due/overdue counts must be computed using indexed date queries (`get_due_overdue_groups` SQL RPC) to prevent performance degradation with large loan tables.

### 3.4 Role and Security Controls

- Access to the `/admin/reminders` route and reminder dispatch Edge Functions is restricted to users with the `admin` role (`FR-AUTH-05`).

## 4. Preconditions

### 4.1 Librarian Authentication

The Librarian (Admin) is logged in with administrative access rights.

### 4.2 Circulation Data Availability

The library database is accessible, and the `loans` and `loan_items` tables contain active circulation records.

---

## 5. Postconditions

### 5.1 Reminders Dispatched

- Notification records are persisted in the `notifications` table for all targeted students.
- Students currently active in the web app receive real-time bell badge increments via Supabase Realtime.

### 5.2 Reminder Timestamps Recorded

- `last_reminded_at` is updated to the current timestamp on all corresponding loan items, establishing the 24-hour cooldown baseline.
