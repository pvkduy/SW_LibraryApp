## 1. Use-Case Name

**UC-29 - Oversee reservation queues** (Traces to: `FR-RES-05`, `FEAT-16`, `FEAT-17`, `TS-40`)

### 1.1 Brief Description

This use case enables a Librarian (Admin) to monitor all active reservation queues across library titles, verify FIFO (First-In, First-Out) waitlist rankings, inspect ready-for-pickup holds with their 48-hour pickup expiration countdowns, and review readers' active reservation strike counts and ban statuses. In operational scenarios such as copy damage or administrative adjustments, staff can cancel a reservation with a mandatory documented reason without penalizing the reader with a strike. When a ready hold is cancelled, the system automatically triggers the promotion engine to allocate the physical copy to the next eligible member in the queue.

### 1.2 Actors

- **Primary Actor:** Librarian (Admin)
- **Supporting Actor:** Reservation Promotion Engine & Notification Service

## 2. Flow of Events

### 2.1 Basic Flow

This use case begins when an authenticated **Librarian (Admin)** opens the Reservation Queues management screen (`/admin/reservations`).

1. The **Librarian (Admin)** navigates to "Reservation Queues" via the back-office navigation menu.
2. The **System** queries active reservations (`waiting` and `ready_for_pickup` statuses) joined with `books`, `copies`, and student `profiles`.
3. The **System** displays the Reservation Queues dashboard grouped by book title:
   - For each book with an active queue: Title, ISBN, total waiting queue depth, total copies, available copies, and held copies.
   - Expandable queue rows showing: Queue Rank (FIFO order), Student Name, Student ID, Reservation Date, Current Status (`waiting` or `ready_for_pickup`), Assigned Copy Barcode (if `ready_for_pickup`), Pickup Expiration Deadline (countdown timer based on 48-hour window), and Student Strike Count / Ban Status.
   - Status filters: "All", "Ready for Pickup", "Waiting in Queue".
   - Search bar (by Title, ISBN, or Student ID).
4. The **Librarian (Admin)** expands a book queue and reviews the queue positions and pickup deadlines.
5. The **Librarian (Admin)** completes queue review and closes the screen. The use case ends.

### 2.2 Alternative Flows

#### 2.2.1 Filter Queue Oversight by Book or Status

_Branching point: At Step 3 of the Basic Flow, if the Librarian filters to inspect specific books or states._

1. The **Librarian (Admin)** enters a book title or student ID in the search input or toggles the status filter to "Ready for Pickup".
2. The **System** filters the queue table to display only matching books or individual reservation items.
3. The **Librarian (Admin)** reviews the filtered list of reservations awaiting shelf handover or student pickup.
4. The flow resumes at Step 4 of the Basic Flow.

#### 2.2.2 Cancel a Ready Reservation with Cause and Promote Next Member

_Branching point: At Step 4 of the Basic Flow, if staff must cancel a held reservation (e.g., the held copy was found damaged or reported misplaced before pickup, or the student phoned to withdraw)._

1. The **Librarian (Admin)** locates a reservation in `ready_for_pickup` status and clicks the "Cancel Reservation" action button.
2. The **System** prompts for confirmation via a modal dialog requiring a mandatory cancellation reason (10 to 500 characters) and explicitly states that staff cancellation does not assign a strike to the student.
3. The **Librarian (Admin)** enters the reason (e.g., _"Copy barcode C-0023 found water damaged during physical inspection; book removed for repair"_) and confirms the cancellation.
4. The **System** executes an atomic Edge Function transaction:
   - Updates the target reservation status to `cancelled` and records `cancelled_by = admin_id`, `cancellation_reason`, and `cancelled_at = NOW()`.
   - Ensures no strike is recorded on the student's profile (`POL-07`).
   - Dispatches an in-app and email notification to the student informing them of the cancellation and the staff reason.
   - If the held copy remains usable (e.g., student withdrew via phone call), the system invokes the **Promotion Engine** (`FR-RES-02`):
     - Locates the next member in the queue with `waiting` status.
     - Transitions the next reservation to `ready_for_pickup`, assigns the copy barcode, and sets a fresh 48-hour pickup deadline (`expires_at = NOW() + 48 hours`).
     - Sends a "Your reserved book is ready for pickup" notification to the newly promoted member.
   - If the copy was retired or marked damaged (`UC-22`), the hold is released and the remaining queue remains in `waiting` status until another copy is returned.
5. The **System** writes an audit log entry documenting the staff cancellation and subsequent promotion (`FR-SYS-03`).
6. The **System** refreshes the queue view, displaying the updated reservation status and next promoted member.
7. The flow resumes at Step 4 of the Basic Flow.

#### 2.2.3 Cancel a Waiting Reservation with Cause

_Branching point: At Step 4 of the Basic Flow, if staff cancels a waiting reservation (e.g., title is being completely retired or made non-circulating)._

1. The **Librarian (Admin)** locates a reservation in `waiting` status and clicks "Cancel Reservation".
2. The **System** prompts for a mandatory cancellation reason in a confirmation dialog.
3. The **Librarian (Admin)** inputs the reason (e.g., _"Title withdrawn from library circulation by academic council"_) and clicks "Confirm Cancel".
4. The **System** transitions the reservation to `cancelled` with the recorded reason, does not issue a strike, and notifies the student.
5. The **System** recalculates queue ranks for the remaining waiting reservations for that book.
6. The flow resumes at Step 4 of the Basic Flow.

#### 2.2.4 Inspect Student Strikes and Ban Eligibility

_Branching point: At Step 3 of the Basic Flow, if the Librarian investigates a student's reservation history._

1. The **Librarian (Admin)** clicks on the student's strike indicator (e.g., "1 strike" or "Banned until 15/Dec/2026") next to their queue entry.
2. The **System** displays a popover detail summarizing the student's strike history within the 180-day rolling window (`reservation.strike_window_days`):
   - Dates of previous hold expirations that caused strikes (`FR-RES-03`).
   - Active reservation ban status and remaining ban days (if strikes equal `strikes_before_ban = 2`, triggering a 30-day ban).
3. The popover provides a deep link: "View Student Detail" (`UC-31`).
4. The **Librarian (Admin)** reviews the history and closes the popover.
5. The flow resumes at Step 4 of the Basic Flow.

#### 2.2.5 Attempted Manual Queue Reordering Blocked by Policy

_Branching point: At Step 3 of the Basic Flow, if staff inquires about or attempts to manually reorder queue priority._

1. The user interface explicitly presents queue ranks as strictly non-editable and immutable, without drag-and-drop or rank override controls (`FR-RES-05`).
2. If an administrative API call attempts to alter queue order directly, the server rejects the request with HTTP 400 (`QUEUE_REORDER_PROHIBITED`).
3. The flow resumes at Step 4 of the Basic Flow.

## 3. Special Requirements

### 3.1 Queue Strict FIFO and Promotion Integrity

- The reservation queue must strictly adhere to FIFO ordering determined by reservation creation timestamp (`created_at ASC`).
- Staff are explicitly prohibited from manually reordering the queue or prioritizing one reader over another (`FR-RES-05`, `Constitution Principle III`).

### 3.2 No Strike on Staff Cancellation

- When a reservation is cancelled by a staff member (`cancelled_by = admin_id`), the system must never assign a penalty strike to the student, even if the reservation was in `ready_for_pickup` status (`FR-RES-05`).

### 3.3 Pickup Deadline and Expiry Processing

- Ready reservations expire automatically 48 hours after promotion (`pickup_window_hours = 48`). The queue oversight screen must show real-time remaining countdowns and highlight reservations within 12 hours of expiration.

### 3.4 Role Security and Audit Tracking

- Only staff accounts with the `admin` role can access `/admin/reservations` or execute staff cancellations.
- Every cancellation and promotion event must write a permanent entry to the system audit trail (`FR-SYS-03`).

## 4. Preconditions

### 4.1 Staff Authentication

The Librarian (Admin) is authenticated with active administrative credentials.

### 4.2 Database Operational State

The PostgreSQL database is online and the reservation engine and background jobs are active.

## 5. Postconditions

### 5.1 Queue Monitored

- The Librarian has inspected the waitlist and pickup hold states.

### 5.2 Cancelled Reservation State Transitioned

- The cancelled reservation is updated to `cancelled`, recording the staff operator ID, timestamp, and explanation reason.
- The affected student is notified via in-app alert.

### 5.3 Next Reservation Promoted Automatically

- If a held physical copy was freed and is eligible, the next waiting reservation is transitioned to `ready_for_pickup` with an updated 48-hour expiration deadline.
