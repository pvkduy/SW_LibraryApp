## 1. Use-Case Name

**UC-21 - Process a book return** (Traces to: `FEAT-12`, `FEAT-13`, `FEAT-21`, `FEAT-22`, `FEAT-23`, `FEAT-24`, `FR-CIRC-03`, `FR-CIRC-04`, `FR-COND-01`, `FR-LOAN-04`, `FR-LOAN-05`, `FR-LOAN-06`, `FR-LOAN-09`, `FR-RES-02`, `POL-08`)

### 1.1 Brief Description

This use case enables a **Librarian (Admin)** to process the physical return of borrowed library books at the circulation desk. The Librarian scans the unique copy barcode (Code 128) to locate the active loan record, inspects the returned physical book, records the incoming condition (`good`, `fair`, `poor`, `damaged`), evaluates overdue fines (if returned past due) and damage fees (if condition degraded), selects the post-return copy routing (`available`, `damaged`, or `under_repair`), and confirms the return. The system updates the loan status to `returned`, writes an inventory transaction, creates necessary charges, optionally processes counter payment, triggers reservation queue promotion if waiting holds exist, and invites the student to rate and review the title.

### 1.2 Actors

- **Primary Actor:** Librarian (Admin)
- **Secondary Human Actor:** Student (Borrower returning books)
- **Supporting Actor:** Notification Service (In-app and Email)

## 2. Flow of Events

### 2.1 Basic Flow

This use case begins when a **Student** brings a borrowed physical book to the library circulation desk for return.

1. The **Librarian** navigates to the "Circulation Desk" at `/admin/circulation` and selects the "Return Book" workstation.
2. The **Librarian** scans the physical book's unique Code 128 copy barcode label using the desk scanner (or types the barcode into the input field).
3. The **System** queries active loans, identifies the corresponding `borrowed` loan item, and retrieves:
   - Borrower's name and Student ID.
   - Book title, author, and replacement value.
   - Borrow date, scheduled due date, and overdue days count (`0 days`).
   - Baseline condition recorded at handover (e.g., `good`) with any attached condition notes or photos.
4. The **Librarian** inspects the returned book against the handover condition and confirms that the book is returned on time without physical degradation.
5. The **Librarian** selects the incoming condition as `good` (matching baseline) and leaves the destination copy status as `available`.
6. The **System** calculates total charges owed for this return as `0 VND` (no overdue fine, no damage fee).
7. The **Librarian** clicks "Confirm Return".
8. The **System** executes an atomic PostgreSQL transaction that:
   - Sets the loan item status to `returned` and stamps `returned_at = now()`.
   - Records the returned condition rating and inspector identity (`librarian.id`) on the loan record.
   - Sets the copy status to `available` (or routes to `held` via [Extension Point 6.2](#62-automatic-reservation-promotion-on-returned-copy) if reservation queue exists).
   - Records an immutable row in `inventory_transactions` with `type = status_change`, `delta = 0`, `reference_id = loan_item.id`, and `reason = "Book return on time"`.
   - Updates book counters (`available_copies = available_copies + 1`).
   - Invokes [Extension Point 6.3](#63-post-return-rating-and-review-invitation) to generate a review invitation for the student.
9. The **System** displays a green success confirmation message: _"Return completed successfully. Copy [Barcode] restored to available collection."_
10. The **System** dispatches a return receipt confirmation notification to the student's email and account notification feed. The use case ends.

### 2.2 Alternative Flows

#### 2.2.1 Overdue Return and Fine Calculation

_Branching point: At Step 3 of the Basic Flow, if the return date is past the scheduled due date._

1. The **System** detects overdue status, computes the number of overdue calendar days (local calendar days elapsed after 23:59:59 on the due date), and calculates the fine per `POL-08`:
   $$\text{Fine} = \min(\text{Overdue Days} \times \text{Daily Rate}, \text{Book Replacement Price})$$
   *(Formula: `Fine = min(Overdue Days * Daily Rate, Book Replacement Price)`)*
   (e.g., 3 days overdue at 2,000 VND/day = 6,000 VND).
2. The **System** checks whether any overdue fine charges were already accrued or pre-paid via nightly batch jobs:
   - If 4,000 VND was previously paid online by the student, the system calculates the remaining incremental fine balance (e.g., 2,000 VND).
3. The **System** displays an overdue notice banner displaying the overdue days, daily rate, and the finalized fine amount.
4. The **Librarian** proceeds to inspect condition (Step 4 of Basic Flow) and confirms return.
5. In Step 8 of Basic Flow, the **System** creates a finalized `overdue_fine` Charge for the unpaid fine balance.
6. The flow branches to [Alternative Flow 2.2.5](#225-immediate-counter-settlement-vs-account-debt-hold) for settlement options.

#### 2.2.2 Physical Condition Worsened and Damage Fee Assessment

_Branching point: At Step 4 of the Basic Flow, if the book exhibits physical damage, torn pages, liquid spills, or marked text not present at handover._

1. The **Librarian** selects an incoming condition that is lower than baseline (e.g., baseline `good` $\rightarrow$ returned `damaged`).
2. The **System** detects condition degradation and forces the Librarian to select a damage fee tier (`minor`, `major`, or `unusable`) or enter a custom amount with mandatory explanation (`DEC-38`):
   - `minor`: 10% of book replacement price.
   - `major`: 30% of book replacement price.
   - `unusable`: 100% of book replacement price.
3. The **Librarian** selects the appropriate tier (e.g., `major` on a 100,000 VND book = 30,000 VND damage fee), inputs an explanatory note, and attaches up to 3 photos of the damaged areas.
4. The **Librarian** selects the destination copy status: `damaged` (sequestered from circulation) or `under_repair` (dispatched to book repair bindery).
5. Upon confirmation (Step 8 of Basic Flow), the **System**:
   - Creates a `damage_fee` Charge row linked to the loan and student.
   - Updates copy status to `damaged` or `under_repair`.
   - Records an inventory transaction detailing the damage classification.
   - Adjusts book counters (`available_copies` is not incremented; `damaged` copies remain non-circulating).
6. The flow branches to [Alternative Flow 2.2.5](#225-immediate-counter-settlement-vs-account-debt-hold) for fee settlement.

#### 2.2.3 Copy Barcode Not On Loan or Already Returned

_Branching point: At Step 3 of the Basic Flow, if the scanned copy barcode is in status `available`, `retired`, `under_repair`, or does not exist in the database._

1. The **System** plays an error chime and displays an alert:
   - _"Copy [Barcode] is currently marked as [Status]. No active borrowed loan found for this copy."_
2. The **System** provides quick action links:
   - View Copy Inventory History (`UC-26`)
   - Check Circulation Log
3. The **Librarian** inspects the physical copy barcode label to ensure it was not misread or confused with an ISBN barcode, or returns the copy to shelving if it was already checked in. The use case ends.

#### 2.2.4 Return of a Book Previously Declared Lost (Path A Recovery)

_Branching point: At Step 3 of the Basic Flow, if the scanned copy is currently in `lost` status (previously marked lost manually or by 60-day auto-conversion)._

1. The **System** recognizes the return of a lost book and initiates the "Lost Book Recovery Workflow (Path A)" (`POL-08`, `DEC-17`):
   - The **System** retrieves the associated lost loan item and student profile (which is currently `restricted`).
   - The **System** automatically cancels/waives the pending `loss_fee` replacement Charge (or marks it for refund if already paid).
   - Accrued overdue fines up to the lost declaration date remain owed.
2. The **Librarian** inspects condition and records incoming condition and destination status.
3. The **System** transitions the copy from `lost` to `available` (or `damaged`/`under_repair`), updates loan status to `returned`, and sets `lost_resolved_at = now()`.
4. If remaining overdue fines exist, the flow proceeds to [Alternative Flow 2.2.5](#225-immediate-counter-settlement-vs-account-debt-hold).
5. Once all fines are satisfied, the student's account restore checklist becomes eligible for staff clearance (`FR-AUTH-08`). The use case ends.

#### 2.2.5 Immediate Counter Settlement vs. Account Debt Hold

_Branching point: During return confirmation when total charges (fines + damage fees) are greater than 0._

1. The **System** displays the total charges breakdown (e.g., 6,000 VND fine + 30,000 VND damage fee = 36,000 VND total owed) and presents two workflow options:
   - "Pay Now at Counter"
   - "Defer Payment (Apply Debt Hold to Account)"
2. **Path A - Counter Payment:**
   - The **Student** tenders cash or QR counter payment.
   - The **Librarian** selects "Pay Now at Counter", verifies the exact amount, and clicks "Confirm Settlement & Return" (`FR-CIRC-07`).
   - The **System** invokes [Extension Point 6.1](#61-immediate-counter-payment) to create Payment and Receipt records, marks the charges `paid`, updates the loan to `returned`, and clears any debt hold.
3. **Path B - Defer Payment:**
   - The **Student** requests to pay online later or lacks immediate funds.
   - The **Librarian** selects "Defer Payment".
   - The **System** closes the loan (`status = returned`), leaves the charges in `unpaid` status, and applies a `debt_hold` flag to the student's profile.
   - The **System** warns that new borrow checkouts and reservations are suspended until the debt is cleared (`FR-LOAN-07`).
4. The use case ends.

## 3. Special Requirements

### 3.1 Auditability and Immutability

- Every return event must document the inspecting staff member (`operator_id`), exact timestamp, incoming condition, and destination copy status.
- Once a return is confirmed and the loan status becomes `returned`, the loan record cannot be reverted to `borrowed`. Condition corrections require an explicit audited correction flow (`FR-COND-01`).

### 3.2 Fine Capping and Calculation Integrity

- Total overdue fines accrued by a single loan item can never exceed the recorded replacement price of the book (`DEC-16`, `POL-08`).
- Calculation must take into account any fine amounts previously paid during the loan period to prevent double-charging.

### 3.3 Automated Promotion Trigger

- Whenever a returned copy is restored to `available` condition and has active reservation queues, the system must trigger queue promotion within the same database transaction (`FR-RES-02`).

### 3.4 Concurrency and Idempotency

- Double-scanning or simultaneous return attempts on the same copy barcode must be protected by database row locks (`SELECT FOR UPDATE`), preventing duplicate returns or orphaned receipts.

## 4. Preconditions

### 4.1 Librarian Authentication

The Librarian is logged into the administrative portal with an active session holding circulation management rights.

### 4.2 Borrowed Item Existence

The physical book copy must currently correspond to an open loan record in status `borrowed` (or `lost` in recovery flow).

---

## 5. Postconditions

### 5.1 Loan Closed and Inventory Restored

- Loan item status is updated to `returned` with recorded return timestamp and condition.
- Physical copy status transitions to `available`, `damaged`, or `held` (if promoted to a waiting reservist).
- Overdue fines and damage fees are either paid (with receipts created) or recorded as unpaid charges with an active debt hold.
- Inventory transactions and denormalized copy counters are updated.

### 5.2 Return Aborted

- If barcode is invalid or staff cancels, no loan state changes or charges are persisted.

## 6. Extension Points

### 6.1 Immediate Counter Payment

- **Location:** Step 2 of Alternative Flow 2.2.5.
- **Trigger:** When student settles fines or damage charges on the spot.
- **Behavior:** Invokes `UC-13 (Pay a charge)` / `UC-23 (Manage receipts and reversals)` to execute counter payment, issue printable receipts, and update charge statuses to `paid`.

### 6.2 Automatic Reservation Promotion on Returned Copy

- **Location:** Step 8 of Basic Flow.
- **Trigger:** When the returned copy transitions to `available` and the book has waiting reservations (`queue_length > 0`).
- **Behavior:** The promotion engine assigns the copy to the next eligible reservist, marks copy as `held`, generates a pickup deadline, and notifies the student.

### 6.3 Post-Return Rating and Review Invitation

- **Location:** Step 8 of Basic Flow.
- **Trigger:** Upon successful return completion.
- **Behavior:** The system creates a pending review prompt accessible in the student's dashboard, enabling `UC-15 (Rate and review a returned book)`.
