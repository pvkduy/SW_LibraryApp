## 1. Use-Case Name

**UC-12 - Renew a loan** (Traces to: `FEAT-11`, `POL-06`, `FR-LOAN-03`)

### 1.1 Brief Description

This use case enables an authenticated Student (Reader) to extend the borrowing duration of an active loan item (`borrowed` state) directly from their "My Loans" dashboard without needing to bring the physical book back to the service desk. The system evaluates current circulation policies (`POL-06`), membership snapshot privileges, remaining renewal allowances, existing debt holds, overdue status, and title reservation demand. If all eligibility criteria are satisfied, the due date is calculated and extended immediately (or following payment confirmation if a fee is required for non-member Premium renewals), updating the loan record and notifying the student.

### 1.2 Actors

- **Primary Actor:** Student (Reader)
- **Supporting Actor:** Payment Provider / Simulator (when renewing fee-bearing loans for non-members)

## 2. Flow of Events

### 2.1 Basic Flow

This flow covers the standard, free renewal of an eligible borrowed book (e.g., standard book loan or an active membership loan).

1. The **Student** navigates to the "My Loans" page (`/loans`) and views the list of ongoing loans.
2. The **System** retrieves the student's active loans, current membership status, outstanding debt balance, and renewal counters. For each loan item currently in `borrowed` status, the **System** checks renewal eligibility per policy `POL-06`:
   - Item status is strictly `borrowed` (not `overdue`).
   - Account status is `active` (not `restricted` or `inactive`).
   - Student has no debt hold (`outstanding_debt == 0`).
   - Renewals used is strictly less than renewals allowed by current entitlements (1 for non-members, 2 for active members).
   - No pending reservation queue exists for the corresponding book (`reservations.status == 'waiting'`).
3. The **System** displays the "Renew" action button enabled next to each eligible loan item, displaying the remaining renewals available (e.g., "1 renewal remaining").
4. The **Student** clicks the "Renew" button for an eligible loan.
5. The **System** presents a confirmation dialog indicating the book title, current due date, the new proposed due date (calculated as current due date + standard entitlement loan duration: +14 days for members, +7 days for non-members), and confirms no fee is required.
6. The **Student** confirms the renewal action.
7. The **System** invokes the renewal transaction:
   - Verifies conditions concurrently under row locking.
   - Increments the `renewals_used` counter on the loan item by 1.
   - Updates `due_date` to `old_due_date + INTERVAL (entitlement_loan_days)`.
   - Records an audit trail log entry for the renewal event.
8. The **System** displays a success notification with the updated due date and refreshes the loan card in the "My Loans" view.
9. The **System** dispatches an in-app notification confirming the successful extension. The use case ends.

### 2.2 Alternative Flows

#### 2.2.1 Renewal Limit Reached

_Branching point: At Step 2 or Step 4 of the Basic Flow, if the loan item's `renewals_used` equals or exceeds the maximum renewals permitted by the current entitlement._

1. The **System** disables the "Renew" button for the specific loan card.
2. The **System** displays an informative tooltip or inline badge: _"Renewal limit reached for this loan (Max: X)."_
3. If the student attempts to trigger renewal via direct API call, the server rejects the request with error code `RENEWAL_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`.
4. The use case ends with no modification to the loan due date.

#### 2.2.2 Loan Already Overdue

_Branching point: At Step 2 or Step 4 of the Basic Flow, if the current timestamp has passed the existing `due_date` (loan is in `overdue` status)._

1. The **System** displays the loan with status badge "Overdue" and shows accrued fines.
2. The "Renew" button is hidden or disabled, replaced with the notice: _"Overdue items cannot be renewed. Please return the book to the library service counter and settle accrued fines."_
3. The use case ends.

#### 2.2.3 Reservation Queue Exists for Title

_Branching point: At Step 2 or Step 4 of the Basic Flow, if another library member has placed a reservation on the title (`waiting` queue length > 0)._

1. The **System** identifies that a waiting reservation exists in `reservations` for the parent `book_id`.
2. The **System** disables the "Renew" button and displays the rationale: _"Cannot renew: Another member is currently waiting in reservation queue for this title."_
3. The student is advised to return the book on or before the current due date so it can circulate to the waiting member.
4. The use case ends.

#### 2.2.4 Account Blocked by Debt Hold or Restricted Status

_Branching point: At Step 2 or Step 4 of the Basic Flow, if the student account has an outstanding debt (`debt_hold = true`) or is in `restricted` status due to unresolved loss/fines._

1. The **System** detects outstanding unpaid charges or a restricted account flag (`POL-09`, `DEC-20`).
2. The **System** disables renewal actions across all loans and displays a persistent warning banner: _"Renewals blocked due to outstanding balance or account restriction. Please settle your charges."_
3. The student can click the link in the banner to navigate directly to `/charges` (`UC-13 - Pay a charge`).
4. The use case ends without extending the loan.

#### 2.2.5 Renewal of Premium Loan by Non-Member (Fee-bearing Flow)

_Branching point: At Step 5 of the Basic Flow, if the book belongs to the `Premium` collection and the student does not hold an active membership (`DEC-06`, `DEC-52`)._

1. The **System** identifies that the loan is for a Premium catalog book and the current user is a non-member.
2. In the confirmation dialog, the **System** informs the student: _"Renewing this Premium title requires an additional rental fee of 5,000 VND. The due date will be extended upon payment confirmation."_
3. The **Student** clicks "Proceed to Payment".
4. The **System** creates a pending renewal charge and redirects the student to the Simulated Payment Gateway (`/pay/:paymentId`) or opens the payment modal.
5. The **Student** completes the payment via the simulated payment interface (`UC-13`).
6. Upon receiving payment success confirmation from the payment handler:
   - The **System** marks the renewal payment as `completed` and creates a financial receipt record.
   - The **System** updates the loan item: increments `renewals_used` by 1 and extends `due_date` by 7 days.
7. The **System** redirects the student back to `/loans` with a confirmation toast: _"Payment successful. Loan renewed until [New Due Date]."_
8. The flow resumes at Step 9 of the Basic Flow.

#### 2.2.6 Simulated Payment Aborted or Failed

_Branching point: At Step 5 of Alternative Flow 2.2.5, if the payment fails, times out, or is cancelled by the student._

1. The **Payment Simulator** reports a transaction failure or the student clicks "Cancel".
2. The **System** displays an alert: _"Renewal payment was not completed. The due date has not been extended."_
3. The pending renewal charge is voided or expired.
4. The original loan item remains in `borrowed` state with its initial `due_date` and unchanged `renewals_used` count.
5. The use case ends.

## 3. Special Requirements

### 3.1 Transaction Integrity and Concurrency

- Loan renewal execution must be atomic and executed within a single database transaction using row-level locking (`SELECT FOR UPDATE` on `loan_items`).
- The system must prevent double-clicks or concurrent requests from using up multiple renewal allowances or extending due dates multiple times simultaneously.

### 3.2 Policy Dynamic Evaluation

- Entitlements (loan extension days, renewal limits) must be evaluated dynamically based on the student's status at the moment of renewal (`DEC-53`).
- If a student had an active membership when borrowing, but the membership has expired before renewal, non-member policies apply (7 days extension, 1 total renewal max).
- Conversely, if a student upgraded to membership while holding an active loan, member renewal benefits (14 days extension, up to 2 renewals) become accessible provided other constraints hold.

### 3.3 Performance and Responsiveness

- Eligibility checks and standard free renewal processing must complete within 500 ms under normal operating load.
- Real-time notification of the renewed due date must reflect immediately on client dashboards.

## 4. Preconditions

### 4.1 Authenticated and Active Session

The Student is authenticated with an `active` account profile and is signed into the ShelfHelp application.

### 4.2 Eligible Ongoing Loan

The Student possesses at least one active loan item in `borrowed` status that has not yet reached its due date (`due_date > NOW()`).

---

## 5. Postconditions

### 5.1 Successful Renewal

- The `loan_items.renewals_used` field is incremented by 1.
- The `loan_items.due_date` timestamp is extended by the appropriate number of days (+14 days for members, +7 days for non-members).
- If fee-bearing, a payment record and valid financial receipt are persisted.
- An in-app confirmation notification is logged and visible in the student's notification center (`/notifications`).

### 5.2 Failed or Blocked Renewal

- The loan item's `due_date` and `renewals_used` remain strictly unmodified.
- No charges or receipts are finalized if the transaction is cancelled or rejected.
- Clear error feedback explains the exact reason why renewal could not proceed.

## 6. Extension Points

### 6.1 Extension to Pay a Charge (UC-13)

- **Condition:** Non-member renewing a Premium collection item.
- **Location:** Step 5 of Alternative Flow 2.2.5. The workflow invokes `UC-13 - Pay a charge` to settle the required per-book renewal fee before completing the due date extension.
