## 1. Use-Case Name

**UC-11 - View loans, charges, and payment history** (Traces to: `FEAT-10`, `FR-LOAN-01`, `FR-LOAN-02`, `FR-LOAN-05`, `FR-LOAN-10`, `DEC-08`, `DEC-09`, `DEC-10`, `DEC-15`, `DEC-16`)

### 1.1 Brief Description

This use case allows an authenticated Student (Reader) to monitor and inspect their library activity across three integrated domains:
1. **Loans and Requests:** Real-time visibility into ongoing requests (`awaiting_payment`, `payment_failed`, `ready_for_pickup`), active loans (`borrowed`, `overdue`), lost book cases (`lost`), and full borrowing history (`returned`, `expired`, `cancelled`). Includes precise pickup countdowns, due dates, and direct action triggers (such as loan renewals).
2. **Charges and Fines:** Clear visibility into unpaid overdue fines, replacement fees, damage assessments, running daily fine calculations (2,000 VND/day capped at replacement price), and overall outstanding debt.
3. **Payment History and Receipts:** Complete, tamper-evident audit ledger of all financial receipts generated for membership purchases, borrowing fees, and fine settlements, including printable electronic receipts and linked reversal entries.

### 1.2 Actors

- **Primary Actor:** Student (Reader)
- **Supporting Systems:** Supabase PostgreSQL Database, Supabase Realtime Engine

## 2. Flow of Events

### 2.1 Basic Flow

#### 2.1.1 Viewing Ongoing and Historical Loans

This sub-flow begins when the **Student** navigates to the My Loans page (`/loans`).

1. The **Student** clicks "My Loans" in the navigation bar.
2. The **System** queries `loan_items`, `loan_requests`, and `reservations` belonging to the authenticated student, joining corresponding `books` and `copies` data.
3. The **System** segments the records into two primary tabs:
   - **Ongoing:** Items in active states (`awaiting_payment`, `payment_failed`, `ready_for_pickup`, `borrowed`, `overdue`, unresolved `lost`, and active/ready reservations).
   - **History:** Completed or closed items (`returned`, `expired`, `cancelled`, resolved `lost`, and fulfilled/cancelled reservations).
4. For each item in the Ongoing view, the **System** renders:
   - Book cover thumbnail, title, author, and physical copy barcode.
   - Exact current status badge (`Awaiting Payment`, `Payment Failed`, `Ready for Pickup`, `Borrowed`, `Overdue`, `Lost`).
   - Relevant temporal markers:
     - For `ready_for_pickup`: Live countdown timer to the pickup deadline (e.g., _"Pickup deadline: 18h 42m remaining"_), styled with high-contrast warning when under 12 hours.
     - For `borrowed`: Due date and remaining loan days.
     - For `overdue`: Overdue day counter, running fine amount, and projected date of conversion to `lost`.
   - Context-sensitive action controls:
     - "View Pickup Code" (linking to `/loans/requests/:requestId`).
     - "Renew Loan" (enabled if eligible under `POL-06`).
     - "Pay Now" (for fee-bearing or overdue items).
5. The **Student** reviews their current obligations and deadlines. The sub-flow ends.

#### 2.1.2 Viewing Charges, Fines, and Incurred Debt

This sub-flow begins when the **Student** navigates to the Charges & Fines page (`/charges`).

1. The **Student** opens the Charges & Fines section or clicks the "Debt Hold" banner on the home screen.
2. The **System** queries `charges` for the current user, calculating total unpaid balances.
3. The **System** displays:
   - Total Outstanding Debt banner (e.g., _"Total Outstanding: 16,000 VND - Debt Hold Active"_).
   - Itemized list of unpaid charges, detailing:
     - Book title and copy barcode.
     - Charge type (`overdue_fine`, `damage_fee`, `loss_fee`).
     - Rate and calculation basis (e.g., _"8 days overdue × 2,000 VND/day"_).
     - Maximum cap indicator (e.g., _"Fine capped at replacement price of 120,000 VND"_).
     - Charge creation timestamp.
   - Selection checkboxes next to each whole charge and a primary "Pay Selected Charges Online" button (`UC-13`).
   - An instructional panel for in-person settlement: _"Or visit the library circulation desk and quote your Student ID to pay in cash."_
4. The **Student** inspects the breakdown of charges. The sub-flow ends.

#### 2.1.3 Viewing Payment History and Receipts

This sub-flow begins when the **Student** inspects their financial transaction history.

1. The **Student** navigates to the "Payment History" section on `/charges` or `/membership`.
2. The **System** retrieves all `receipts` linked to the student's `user_id`, ordered chronologically (newest first).
3. The **System** renders a searchable, paginated ledger table showing:
   - Receipt Number (e.g., `RCP-20261108-0042`).
   - Issuance Date & Time (formatted in local time `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh`).
   - Transaction Type (`membership_fee`, `loan_fee`, `overdue_fine`, `damage_fee`, `loss_fee`).
   - Payment Method (`online` or `counter`).
   - Amount paid in VND.
   - Status (`Valid`, `Reversed`, or `Voided`).
   - "View Receipt" action button.
4. The **Student** clicks "View Receipt" on a specific entry.
5. The **System** displays a clean, printable electronic receipt modal containing full university library branding, transaction metadata, linked book or membership reference, payment identifier, and a "Print / Save PDF" button. The sub-flow ends.

### 2.2 Alternative Flows

#### 2.2.1 Filter and Search Specific Loan Records

_Branching point: At Step 3 of Sub-flow 2.1.1, if the student has extensive borrowing history and wishes to locate a specific title._

1. The **Student** enters a book title or author in the filter box or selects a status filter chip (e.g., `Returned`, `Overdue`, `Cancelled`).
2. The **System** filters the displayed cards client-side or re-queries with pagination (`20 per page`).
3. The **System** displays only matching loan records.
4. The flow resumes at Step 4 of Sub-flow 2.1.1.

#### 2.2.2 Inspecting a Reversed or Refunded Receipt

_Branching point: At Step 4 of Sub-flow 2.1.3, if the student views a receipt that was subject to an administrative cancellation or refund._

1. The **System** retrieves the target receipt and detects a non-null `reversal_receipt_id`.
2. The **System** displays the original receipt marked with a prominent `Reversed` watermark.
3. The **System** renders an embedded link to the corresponding Reversal Receipt, displaying the refund timestamp, administrative reason code, and negative offsetting amount (`FR-RCPT-03`).
4. The **Student** inspects the audit trail. The use case ends.

#### 2.2.3 Real-Time Loan State Mutation While Viewing

_Branching point: While the student has the My Loans page open, an automated background job transitions an item (e.g., ready pickup expires, or book transitions to overdue)._

1. The **System** receives a Supabase Realtime postgres changes event on the user's `loan_items` record.
2. The **System** seamlessly animates the status update on the card (e.g., `Ready for Pickup` transitions to `Expired`, or `Borrowed` transitions to `Overdue`).
3. If an action button was visible (such as "Renew"), the **System** disables or re-evaluates the button according to the new status.
4. A brief toast notification informs the user: _"Loan status updated."_ The flow resumes at Step 4 of Sub-flow 2.1.1.

## 3. Special Requirements

### 3.1 Real-Time Fine Computation and Cap Display

- Daily overdue fines must be calculated at the standard library rate of 2,000 VND per day past the due date (`POL-01`, `DEC-15`).
- The running fine displayed to the student must be strictly capped at the book's recorded replacement price (`replacement_price`).
- Nightly cron jobs (`accrue-overdue-fines`) update the persistent `charges` table; the UI must dynamically display any additional uncommitted days between scheduled runs so the student sees an accurate, up-to-the-minute balance (`DEC-16`, `FR-LOAN-05`).

### 3.2 Client-Server Clock Skew Correction

- Pickup countdowns and payment countdowns must not rely on the client device's local clock, which may be inaccurate or manipulated (`FR-LOAN-02`).
- The system must calculate remaining seconds using server-provided timestamps and offset calculations, maintaining clock synchronization accuracy within 5 seconds.

### 3.3 Security, RLS, and Receipt Auditability

- All queries for loans, charges, and receipts must be guarded by strict PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (`auth.uid() = user_id`). Under no circumstances may a student view or access another patron's pickup codes, loans, or financial receipts.
- Receipt rows are strictly immutable (`DEC-10`, `FR-RCPT-06`). Once created, a receipt cannot be edited or deleted; corrections are handled exclusively via reversal receipts.

## 6. Extension Points

### 6.1 Direct Loan Renewal

- From the Ongoing loans view (`borrowed` item), the student may click "Renew Loan", branching to `UC-12 (Renew a loan)`.

### 6.2 Direct Charge Settlement

- From the Charges & Fines view, the student may select unpaid items and click "Pay Now", branching to `UC-13 (Pay a charge)`.

### 6.3 Post-Return Rating and Review

- From the History loans view (`returned` item), the student may click "Rate & Review", branching to `UC-15 (Rate and review a returned book)`.
