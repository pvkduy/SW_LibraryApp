## 1. Use-Case Name

**UC-27 - Review analytics and export reports** (Traces to: `FEAT-25`, `FR-ANLY-01`, `FR-ANLY-02`, `FR-ANLY-03`, `FR-ANLY-04`, `TS-54`, `TS-55`)

### 1.1 Brief Description

This use case allows a Librarian (Admin) to monitor real-time library operations, circulation velocity, financial metrics, and reader demand signals through an executive analytics dashboard, as well as export structured administrative reports. The dashboard aggregates Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) including active loans versus total stock, overdue counts, net revenue breakdowns (membership plans, premium borrowing fees, overdue fines), and copy write-offs/losses. Staff can analyze circulation rankings (top 10 most and least borrowed titles, category trends), inspect unsatisfied reader demand (top suggestion clusters, long reservation queues, wishlist counts), and export comprehensive operational datasets to CSV, PDF, or Excel formats for institutional compliance.

### 1.2 Actors

- **Primary Actor:** Librarian (Admin)
- **Supporting Actor:** Analytics & Reporting Engine (PostgreSQL RPC Functions & Export Formatter)

## 2. Flow of Events

### 2.1 Basic Flow

This use case begins when an authenticated **Librarian (Admin)** navigates to the Analytics Dashboard (`/admin/dashboard`) or Reports section (`/admin/reports`).

1. The **Librarian (Admin)** opens the "Analytics Dashboard" from the admin portal menu.
2. The **System** sets the default date range filter to the last 30 days (`30 days`).
3. The **System** invokes the database SQL RPC functions (`get_dashboard_kpis`, `get_rankings`, `get_demand_signals`) over indexed transaction tables.
4. The **System** renders the Dashboard KPIs and analytics sections:
   - **Summary KPI Cards:** Items on loan (`borrowed` + `overdue`) vs Total active stock; overdue item count; net fines collected; net membership revenue; net premium-loan fee revenue; damaged copies; and lost copies.
   - **Circulation Rankings & Trends:** Top 10 most borrowed books; bottom 10 least borrowed titles (including titles with zero loans in range); and monthly loan distribution chart grouped by Dewey Decimal / Subject category.
   - **Demand Signals:** Top student suggestion clusters with demand headcount; titles with reservation queue depth $\ge 3$; and highest wishlisted books.
5. The **Librarian (Admin)** reviews the displayed metrics, charts, and demand indicators.
6. The **Librarian (Admin)** closes the dashboard or navigates to other administrative tasks. The use case ends.

### 2.2 Alternative Flows

#### 2.2.1 Change Analytics Date Range Preset or Custom Interval

_Branching point: At Step 4 of the Basic Flow, if the Librarian selects a different analytical timeframe._

1. The **Librarian (Admin)** selects a timeframe preset: "Today", "7 days", "30 days", or selects "Custom Range" to specify exact start and end dates.
2. The **System** validates that the start date precedes or equals the end date.
3. The **System** re-executes the analytics RPCs using the new date parameters.
4. The **System** updates all KPI figures, ranking tables, and category trend charts in place without a complete page reload.
5. The flow resumes at Step 5 of the Basic Flow.

#### 2.2.2 Investigate Demand Signals and Jump to Management Screens

_Branching point: At Step 4 of the Basic Flow, if the Librarian identifies books with high unfulfilled demand or long reservation queues._

1. The **Librarian (Admin)** clicks on an item in the Demand Signals section:
   - Clicking a high-demand Suggestion Group navigates directly to Suggestion Review (`/admin/suggestions`) focused on that title.
   - Clicking a book with an extended queue ($\ge 3$ waiting members) navigates to Reservation Queues (`/admin/reservations`) filtered by that book (`UC-29`).
   - Clicking a popular wishlisted book opens Book Metadata Editor (`/admin/books/:id`) to review copy allocation.
2. The **System** redirects the Librarian to the designated back-office screen with pre-filled filter parameters.
3. The use case ends, transitioning into the respective administrative use case.

#### 2.2.3 Configure and Export Detailed Operational Reports

_Branching point: At Step 1 or Step 5 of the Basic Flow, if the Librarian navigates to the dedicated Reports Export screen (`/admin/reports`)._

1. The **Librarian (Admin)** navigates to the "Reports" sub-menu (`/admin/reports`).
2. The **System** presents a report configuration panel allowing the Librarian to select:
   - Report Type: "Loans & Circulation", "Overdue Loans", "Fines & Payments", "Revenue Ledger", "Inventory & Stocktake", "Book Suggestions", or "Reader Demand Signals".
   - Filters: Date range, book category / faculty, loan status, or payment method.
   - Output Format: CSV, PDF, or Excel (.xlsx).
3. The **Librarian (Admin)** selects the desired report parameters and clicks "Preview Report".
4. The **System** fetches a paginated sample of the first 20 records and calculates total matching row count.
5. The **Librarian (Admin)** reviews the preview table and clicks "Export Report".
6. The **System** verifies that total rows do not exceed the threshold (`export_max_rows = 20,000`).
7. The **System** compiles the dataset, prepends the standardized audit header (`Generated By`, `Timestamp`, `Filter Criteria`, `Row Count`), and delivers the downloaded file to the browser.
8. The flow resumes at Step 5 of the Basic Flow.

#### 2.2.4 Export Prevented Due to Row Count Limit

_Branching point: At Step 6 of Alternative Flow 2.2.3, if the query returns more than 20,000 rows._

1. The **System** detects that the dataset size exceeds `export_max_rows` (20,000 records).
2. The **System** suppresses file generation and displays a warning dialog: _"The requested report contains {N} records, which exceeds the maximum export limit of 20,000 rows. Please narrow your date range or apply specific category filters."_
3. The **Librarian (Admin)** adjusts filter parameters (e.g., shortening the date interval from one year to one quarter).
4. The flow resumes at Step 4 of Alternative Flow 2.2.3.

#### 2.2.5 Handling Sparse or Empty Data Periods

_Branching point: At Step 3 of the Basic Flow or Step 3 of Alternative Flow 2.2.1, if no loan, payment, or inventory activity occurred within the selected window._

1. The **System** detects zero records for the selected date interval.
2. The **System** displays 0 for numeric KPI counters (e.g., `0 VND`, `0 loans`, `0 overdue items`) rather than error states or undefined values.
3. Ranking tables and charts display friendly contextual empty placeholders: _"No circulation activity recorded for this period."_
4. The flow resumes at Step 5 of the Basic Flow.

## 3. Special Requirements

### 3.1 Financial Calculation and Revenue Integrity

- Revenue figures displayed on dashboard cards and financial export reports must represent net signed sums computed from immutable `receipts` records (`DEC-41`).
- Reversals and refunds must be properly factored as negative amounts; if an initial 5,000 VND fee receipt is later reversed via a reversal receipt, the net revenue reflected for that transaction must be exactly 0 VND.

### 3.2 Performance and SQL RPC Execution

- High-level KPI aggregations must execute directly within PostgreSQL via optimized SQL stored procedures (`get_dashboard_kpis`, `get_rankings`) taking advantage of table indexes.
- Dashboard KPI load time must meet the system requirement of $p95 \le 1.0\text{ s}$ (`NFR-04`).

### 3.3 Role-Based Access Control

- All routes under `/admin/dashboard` and `/admin/reports`, along with underlying analytical Edge Functions and RPC endpoints, are strictly protected by RLS and role checks restricting access to users with `admin` metadata (`FR-AUTH-05`).

### 3.4 Report Internationalization

- PDF exports must embed Unicode fonts compliant with UTF-8 Vietnamese character encodings, ensuring correct visual display of diacritics in book titles, student names, and administrative terms.

## 4. Preconditions

### 4.1 Librarian Authentication

The Librarian has authenticated with valid staff credentials possessing the `admin` role.

### 4.2 System Health and Reporting Engine

The database instance is active, and circulation, inventory, and receipt tables are indexed and available.

## 5. Postconditions

### 5.1 Metrics Inspected

- The Librarian has inspected operational statistics and demand indicators.
- No database records are modified as a consequence of viewing analytics.

### 5.2 Audit Entry Generated for Sensitive Exports

- When a comprehensive report export containing financial or circulation data is dispatched, the system logs an audit entry recording the admin user ID, timestamp, report type, applied filters, and row count (`FR-SYS-03`).
