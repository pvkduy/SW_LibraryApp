## 5. Assessment

**Where AI added real value**

- **Cross-file search and propagation.** Each formatting, region, terminology, or REC fix touched 2–4 C4/deployment documents simultaneously; the AI located and updated every occurrence consistently.
- **Consistency checking.** Diffing documented claims against migrations, Edge Functions, `package.json`, `requirements.md`, and `.mmd` sources surfaced discrepancies that are easy to miss by hand.

**Where AI was unreliable**

- **Over-eager deletions and meta-annotations.** The AI added unwanted implementation-tracking callouts (Session 1), prematurely removed `react-easy-crop` and `recharts` from `SHELFHELP_FULLSPEC.md` (Session 4), and stripped backticks when removing hyperlinks (Session 7).
- **Obtrusive qualification prose.** When qualifying planned features, it initially inserted repetitive "PA5 target" callouts that disrupted document readability (Session 6).
- **Model and prompt hallucination in the first report draft.** The AI invented model names (`Gemini 2.5 Pro/Flash`) and prompt text until corrected against the JSONL transcript (Session 5).
- **Sandbox permission boundary.** File deletion (`vision-document/Changes.md`) failed silently in the sandbox until retried outside it.

**Division of responsibility.** Every architectural, scope, migration-strategy, and presentation decision was mine. AI outputs entered the repository only after I reviewed the changes and corrected inaccuracies, tone issues, and over-eager edits.
