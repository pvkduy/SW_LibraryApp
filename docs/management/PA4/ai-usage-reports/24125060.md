## 5. Assessment

**Where AI added real value**

- **Cross-file search and propagation.** Each REC fix touched 2–4 files simultaneously.
  The AI found every occurrence across files faster than a manual grep would have.
- **Consistency checking.** The reconciliation pass required diffing documented claims
  against source files across migrations, Edge Functions, and package.json — work that
  is easy to do incompletely by hand.

**Where AI was unreliable**

- **Model and prompt hallucination in the first draft of this report.** The AI invented
  plausible-sounding model names (`Gemini 2.5 Pro/Flash`) and prompt text that did not
  appear in the transcript. The error was caught immediately on review (prompt 12) and
  corrected by sourcing both directly from the JSONL log.
- **Sandbox permission boundary.** The AI attempted to delete `vision-document/Changes.md`
  within the sandbox and was silently blocked; it required a second attempt outside the
  sandbox to succeed.

**Division of responsibility.** Every architectural, migration-strategy, and
documentation-scope decision was mine. The AI's output entered the project only after I
reviewed each change. Where it made errors (hallucinated report content, sandbox
failure), I caught them through direct inspection.
